Privacy Policy for App

Effective Date: October 2026

FT PVT. LIMITED ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") operates the App mobile application ("App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, collected, processed, used, stored, and shared when you use the App.

We are committed to handling user information responsibly and in accordance with applicable privacy requirements and Google Play policies.

1. Information We Access or Process

The App provides translation, camera translation, voice-related functionality, document/PDF translation, OCR/text recognition, language identification, dictionary, pronunciation, AI-assisted features, history/favorites, advertising, notifications, and premium or in-app purchase functionality.

Depending on the features you use, the following information may be accessed or processed.

Text and Translation Content

When you enter or paste text for translation, dictionary lookup, or AI-assisted functionality, the text is processed to provide the requested service.

Some translation or AI functions may require the text to be transmitted over the internet to a third-party service or processing server.

The App contains integration with Google translation-related services and an external AI processing service.

Images and Camera Content

If you use camera translation or image-based text recognition, the App may access:

images captured using your device camera;

images selected by you from your device; and

text detected within those images.

Camera and media access is used only when required for the feature selected by the user.

Images may be processed using text-recognition/OCR technology.

The App does not require access to your precise GPS location in order to use camera translation.

Voice and Microphone Data

If you use a voice or speech-related feature, the App may request access to your device microphone.

Microphone access is used to receive speech or voice input necessary for the requested functionality.

The App does not intentionally use microphone access for continuous background recording.

Depending on the speech-processing service available on the device or used by the selected feature, speech information may be processed by Android, Google, or another speech-processing provider.

Files and Documents

If you choose a PDF or another supported document for translation or processing, the App may access that document using Android's file-selection functionality.

The App accesses files selected by the user for the purpose of providing the requested document-related functionality.

Translation History and Favorites

The App contains translation history and favorites functionality.

Information saved through these features may be stored locally on your device.

If cloud synchronization or remote storage is enabled in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Device, Advertising, and Technical Information

The App contains advertising, analytics, Firebase, and measurement technologies that may automatically process information such as:

IP address;

general or approximate location inferred from IP address;

advertising identifier;

app-set or installation identifiers;

device model;

operating-system information;

application version;

language or locale;

network information;

advertising impressions and interactions;

app launches, taps and other app interactions;

diagnostic and performance information; and

information used for fraud prevention, security and advertising measurement.

The App does not request Android precise-location or approximate-location permission. Any general location used by advertising services may instead be inferred from technical information such as the device's IP address.

Notification Information

The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging technology and may process an app-instance or installation identifier, application version and related technical information required to deliver notifications.

Purchase Information

The App contains Google Play Billing functionality for premium features, subscriptions, or other in-app purchases.

Google Play may process payment information, transaction details and purchase credentials.

The App may receive information such as:

purchased product or subscription;

purchase status;

purchase token;

entitlement status; and

transaction-related identifiers.

FT PVT. LIMITED does not directly receive users' complete debit-card or credit-card numbers through Google Play Billing.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

provide text and language translation;

recognize text in photographs or images;

provide camera translation;

provide voice or speech-related functionality;

translate or process selected documents;

provide dictionary and pronunciation functionality;

provide AI-assisted responses where requested;

identify languages;

remember history, favorites and user preferences;

download required language models;

deliver push notifications;

provide and manage advertisements;

manage consent choices for advertising where required;

process premium purchases or subscriptions;

measure application and advertising performance;

detect errors and technical problems;

prevent fraud, abuse and invalid advertising activity;

improve stability, reliability and functionality; and

comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell user-created translation content directly as a commercial database.

Advertising and analytics providers may independently process technical, advertising and device information according to their own privacy policies and applicable consent choices.

3. App Permissions

The App may request the following Android permissions.

Camera

Used for camera-based translation, capturing an image, and OCR/text recognition.

Microphone

Used when you choose voice or speech-input functionality.

Photos, Images, Audio, Video and Storage

The App contains media and storage permissions for accessing files or media required by features such as image translation and document processing.

On supported Android versions, Android's system file or media picker may also be used so that you can select specific content.

The App does not intentionally scan unrelated personal files for advertising purposes.

Internet

Required for online translation services, AI functionality, advertisements, Firebase services, notifications, model downloads, dictionaries, purchases and other network-based features.

Network and Wi-Fi State

Used to determine network availability and may also be used by advertising and service SDKs.

Notifications

Used to display notifications when you allow notification permission.

Advertising ID and Android Advertising Services

The App contains advertising technologies that may use the Android Advertising ID and Android advertising-related APIs for advertising, attribution, measurement and fraud prevention, subject to Android settings, consent requirements and applicable law.

Wake Lock / Foreground Processing

May be used to allow certain network or background operations to complete reliably.

Google Play Billing

Used to enable purchases, subscriptions or premium functionality through Google Play.

You may deny optional runtime permissions through Android settings. Some features may not function if their required permission is denied.

5. Analytics, Firebase and Technical Services

The App contains Firebase and Google service components including technologies associated with:

Firebase Analytics;

Firebase Cloud Messaging;

Firebase Installations;

Firebase Crashlytics;

Firebase Database/Firestore libraries;

Google data transport;

Google measurement services; and

Google Install Referrer.

These technologies may process device, installation, application-interaction, notification, diagnostic or technical information depending on the feature and configuration in use.

Google services are subject to Google's privacy policies and service-specific terms.

The App contains Google ML Kit and related machine-learning components for functionality such as:

The App may also communicate with online translation services when an online translation feature is requested.

Information submitted to a third-party service is subject to that service provider's privacy and security practices.

The App contains an AI-chat or contextual-response integration.

When you voluntarily use an AI feature, text or prompts that you submit may be transmitted to the service used to generate a response.

Do not submit confidential, highly sensitive, financial, medical, authentication, password, or other information that is not necessary to use the feature.

The exact independent retention and processing practices of the third-party AI service are controlled by that service provider.
