A few useful answers

Before you open your next file.

Which file formats can I read?

All Document Reader supports PDF, DOC, DOCX, XLS, XLSX, PPT, PPTX and TXT. File complexity, encryption or damage can affect how an individual file opens or displays.

Is All Document Reader available for iPhone?

All Document Reader is an Android app. We have not announced an iPhone version. This website works on Android, iPhone, tablet and desktop browsers.

Are my documents uploaded to your servers?

Core document reading takes place on your device. FT PVT. LIMITED does not operate a document-upload server for the app. Third-party services handle certain technical, analytics and advertising information. Read the app privacy policy for the full details.

Do I need an account?

An FT PVT. LIMITED account is not required for the main document-reading features. File access depends on the permissions you grant on your device.

Does the app contain advertisements?

The free version displays advertising. A paid subscription provides an ad-free experience; non-advertising services such as analytics may remain active. Review the price and billing details shown in Google Play before subscribing.

What if a document will not open?

Check that the file uses a supported format, has downloaded completely and is accessible to the app. If the issue continues, contact support with the file extension, app version and Android version. Do not send confidential documents.
