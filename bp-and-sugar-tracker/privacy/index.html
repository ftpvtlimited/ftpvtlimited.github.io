1. Introduction

Welcome to BP and Sugar Tracker, an Android application developed and provided by FT PVT. LIMITED.

We respect your privacy and recognize the importance of protecting personal and health-related information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the application allows you to enter, how that information is stored and processed, which device features are used, and how you can manage your information.

BP and Sugar Tracker is designed to help users maintain personal health records, track health measurements, manage medication information, and set reminders.

The application primarily operates using local device storage.

This Privacy Policy applies to the BP and Sugar Tracker Android application, version 1.0.0.

2. Information You Provide

BP and Sugar Tracker allows users to enter health-related information manually.

Depending on the features used, the application supports the following information.

A. Blood Pressure Information

  • Systolic blood pressure
  • Diastolic blood pressure
  • Pulse and heart rate
  • Measurement date and time
  • Additional measurement notes

B. Other Health Measurements

  • Blood glucose measurements
  • Weight measurements
  • HbA1c measurements
  • Measurement dates and related notes

C. Medication Information

  • Medication name
  • Medication strength
  • Medication form
  • Medication schedules
  • Medication-related notes

D. Optional Profile Information

  • Name
  • Birth year
  • Doctor's name
  • Next appointment date

This information is provided voluntarily when users choose to use the relevant features.

The application stores these records locally on the user's device.

The analyzed application does not contain an identified account registration or cloud synchronization service.

3. How Your Information Is Used

The application processes the information you enter to provide its supported functions.

These functions include:

  • Saving and displaying blood pressure readings
  • Maintaining a history of health measurements
  • Organizing health information by date
  • Displaying recorded health data
  • Managing medication information
  • Scheduling health and medication reminders
  • Preparing health records for export or sharing
  • Managing optional application security settings

Health information is processed through the application's local functionality.

The analyzed APK does not declare Android's INTERNET permission, and no integration for transmitting health records to FT PVT. LIMITED servers was identified.

4. Local Data Storage

BP and Sugar Tracker uses an on-device database to maintain health information, medication records, preferences, and related application settings.

Information entered into the application is stored locally.

The analyzed version does not contain an identified application cloud backup or account synchronization feature.

The availability of local information may be affected if users:

  • Clear application storage
  • Uninstall the application
  • Reset their device
  • Lose access to the device

Android's device-level backup and restore settings may also affect the availability of application data.

Information exported or shared separately may remain in the selected destination.

5. Notifications and Medication Reminders

BP and Sugar Tracker includes local notification functionality for reminders.

Users can use reminder features to manage medication schedules or health measurement activities.

To support these features, the application declares Android permissions associated with notifications, device restart handling, vibration, exact alarms, and battery optimization settings.

These include:

POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Allows the application to request permission to display notifications on supported Android versions.

RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Supports restoring scheduled reminders after a device restart.

VIBRATE

Supports vibration associated with notifications.

SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM

Supports scheduling reminders at specific times where permitted by Android.

REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS

Allows the application to request access to Android battery optimization settings where appropriate.

Users can manage notification access and related settings through their Android device settings.

These permissions support local reminder functionality and do not, by themselves, establish external transmission of personal health records.

6. App Lock and Biometric Authentication

BP and Sugar Tracker supports optional application security features.

The application includes functionality associated with PIN protection and Android biometric authentication.

The following permissions are declared:

  • USE_BIOMETRIC
  • USE_FINGERPRINT

If biometric authentication is enabled, authentication is performed through supported Android device authentication mechanisms.

The application does not indicate that it collects or stores fingerprint images, facial images, or biometric templates.

The application also contains local PIN-related functionality, including storage of a PIN hash.

These features are intended to restrict unauthorized access to application information on the device.

7. Exporting, Printing, and Sharing Information

BP and Sugar Tracker supports functionality for exporting and sharing health records.

Depending on the feature selected, the application can prepare information for CSV export, report sharing, or printing.

When users choose to share or print information, Android may provide available applications or printing services.

The selected external application, recipient, or printing service may receive the information that the user chooses to provide.

For example, users may choose to save an exported file or share a report through an application available on their device.

Any subsequent handling by that selected destination is subject to its own privacy practices.

Exporting or sharing health information is a user-controlled activity.

9. Advertising and Analytics

The analyzed BP and Sugar Tracker APK does not contain identified integrations for:

  • Google AdMob
  • Advertising mediation networks
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging

Accordingly, this Privacy Policy does not describe advertising-related data collection or processing that has not been detected.

10. Data Sharing and Disclosure

BP and Sugar Tracker primarily maintains user-entered health records in local device storage.

The analyzed APK does not contain an identified developer-operated service for uploading health records to a remote server.

The application does support optional export, sharing, and printing activities.

Information that users choose to export or share may become accessible to their selected destination applications, printing services, or recipients.

Users should review the privacy practices of their selected services before sharing sensitive health information.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Health information entered into BP and Sugar Tracker is maintained in the application's local storage.

The application does not contain an identified developer-operated account or remote health-record database.

Users can remove local application information by clearing the application's data through Android device settings.

Uninstalling the application generally removes its app-specific local storage.

However, Android backup and restore functionality may affect retained copies of application information.

Files previously exported, printed, or shared are not automatically deleted when application data is removed.

Users are responsible for managing copies saved outside the application.

12. Your Privacy Choices

BP and Sugar Tracker provides users with control over information they choose to enter and supported optional features.

Users can:

  • Choose what health information to record
  • Manage supported reminder settings
  • Enable or disable optional application lock features
  • Manage Android notification permissions
  • Control whether information is exported or shared
  • Clear application data using Android device settings
  • Uninstall the application

Certain reminder functionality may be affected when notification or alarm-related permissions are restricted.

13. Health Information and Application Purpose

BP and Sugar Tracker is intended to help users maintain and organize personal health records.

The application supports manual recording and viewing of health-related measurements.

The information displayed by the application is based on the available records and supported application functionality.

BP and Sugar Tracker is not a replacement for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Users should consult qualified healthcare professionals regarding medical conditions, treatment decisions, or concerns about blood pressure and other health measurements.
