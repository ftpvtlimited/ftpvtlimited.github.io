FT PVT. LIMITED respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Edge Glow & Flash Alerts accesses and uses information required for its notification-light and edge-glow functionality.

This Privacy Policy is based on the functionality and permissions present in the analyzed version of the application.

1. Information We Collect

Edge Glow & Flash Alerts does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or address.

The analyzed version of the application does not declare the Android INTERNET permission. Therefore, the application itself does not have normal Internet access for transmitting app data to external servers.

No advertising, analytics, attribution, crash-reporting, or tracking SDK was detected in the analyzed APK.

2. Notification Access

Edge Glow & Flash Alerts uses Android's Notification Listener Service to detect when notifications are posted on your device.

Notification access is required so the app can determine when to display an edge glow, notification light, flash effect, or other configured visual alert.

When you enable Notification Access, Android may make notification information available to the app, including:

  • The application that generated the notification
  • Notification events and status
  • Notification metadata made available by Android
  • Notification content that Android exposes to a Notification Listener Service

The app uses notification information locally on your device for its notification alert functionality.

The analyzed APK does not declare Internet access, and no external analytics or data-transmission SDK was detected. Notification titles or messages are not transmitted to FT PVT. LIMITED or to an external server by this version of the application.

Notification Access is controlled by Android and must be enabled by you through your device settings.

Android's official documentation regarding Notification Listener Service is available at:

https://developer.android.com/reference/android/service/notification/NotificationListenerService

3. Per-App Notification Rules

Edge Glow & Flash Alerts includes functionality that allows users to configure notification or glow behavior for individual applications.

For this feature, the app may access information about applications available on the device, such as an application's package identifier, name, or icon, where Android permits such access.

This information is used locally to display and apply your per-app settings.

The analyzed APK does not contain the QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission.

4. Overlay Permission

The application declares:

android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW

This permission allows Edge Glow & Flash Alerts to display its glow or edge-lighting interface over other applications.

Android requires users to explicitly allow this special permission through system settings.

The permission is used for the app's visual edge-glow functionality and does not itself provide access to the contents of other applications.

Official Android documentation:

https://developer.android.com/reference/android/Manifest.permission#SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW

5. Notification Permission

The application declares:

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

On supported Android versions, this permission allows the application to display its own notifications.

You may allow or deny this permission through the Android permission dialog or device settings.

Official Android documentation:

https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/notifications/notification-permission

6. Lock-Screen and Full-Screen Alert Functionality

The application declares:

android.permission.USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT

The application contains functionality for showing edge-glow alerts on or around the lock screen and may use Android's full-screen notification capabilities when supported and permitted by the operating system.

This functionality may also be used to request that the screen become visible when an applicable notification arrives.

Availability of this feature depends on your Android version, device manufacturer, and system settings.

7. Battery Optimization

The application declares:

android.permission.REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS

Edge Glow & Flash Alerts may direct you to Android system settings where you can choose whether the application should be excluded from certain battery optimizations.

This is intended to help notification-related functionality operate reliably when the app is not open.

The decision to allow this setting remains under your control.

8. Start After Device Reboot

The application declares:

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

This allows the application to receive a system event after the device finishes starting.

It may be used to restore or maintain previously selected Edge Glow & Flash Alerts settings or related functionality after a reboot.

This permission does not provide access to your personal files, messages, contacts, microphone, camera, or location.

9. Local Settings

The application uses local Android storage components to retain app preferences and configuration.

This may include settings such as:

  • Whether glow functionality is enabled
  • Selected glow style
  • Selected colors or palettes
  • Emoji or animation selections
  • Flash preferences
  • Lock-screen preferences
  • Per-app rules
  • Other Edge Glow & Flash Alerts configuration choices

These settings are stored locally on your device.

You can generally remove locally stored app information by clearing the application's storage through Android settings or by uninstalling the application.

10. Information and Permissions Not Detected

Based on the analyzed APK, Edge Glow & Flash Alerts does not declare permissions for:

  • Precise or approximate location
  • Camera
  • Microphone or audio recording
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • External file or storage access

The analyzed APK also did not contain detected integrations for:

  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Firebase Remote Config
  • Google ML Kit
  • Meta/Facebook advertising SDKs
  • AppLovin
  • Mintegral
  • AppsFlyer
  • Adjust

Therefore, this Privacy Policy does not claim that Edge Glow & Flash Alerts uses those permissions or services.

11. Advertising and Analytics

No advertising SDK, advertising mediation SDK, analytics SDK, attribution SDK, or advertising identifier integration was detected in the analyzed version of Edge Glow & Flash Alerts.

The app also does not declare the Android INTERNET permission.

Accordingly, this version of the application is not described in this Privacy Policy as sending usage analytics or advertising information to external providers.

12. Data Sharing

Based on the analyzed APK, Edge Glow & Flash Alerts does not contain an identified external backend, advertising service, analytics service, or tracking service to which notification or app-usage information is transmitted.

The notification-related processing described above is performed locally on the device.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Configuration and preference information used by Edge Glow & Flash Alerts may remain locally on your device while the application is installed.

Because the analyzed version does not contain an identified external user-account or backend storage system, no server-side user account deletion process is required for the functionality detected in the APK.

You can remove the application's locally stored information by:

  • Clearing the application's data from Android Settings, or
  • Uninstalling Edge Glow & Flash Alerts from your device.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the application's actual privacy-related functionality changes in a future version—for example, if FT PVT. LIMITED adds an external service or functionality that processes additional information—this Privacy Policy should be updated to accurately reflect those changes.

The effective date shown at the top of this Privacy Policy identifies the version currently in effect.
