1. Introduction

FT PVT. LIMITED ("we," "us," or "our") provides EdgeGlow & 3D Wallpaper (the "App"). We respect your privacy and aim to provide clear information about how the App handles information and uses third-party services.

This Privacy Policy explains our App's functionality, device permissions, local data processing, third-party services, and privacy choices.

2. About the App

EdgeGlow & 3D Wallpaper is a mobile personalization application offering features such as:

  • Edge lighting and screen border effects.
  • 3D wallpapers and visual customization.
  • Notification-responsive lighting.
  • Camera flash notification effects.
  • Customizable colors and lighting styles.

Certain features may require Android permissions or special system access.

3. Information Collection and Processing

The App's core personalization features process information locally on your device.

FT PVT. LIMITED does not collect, upload, or store personal information from the App on its own servers.

Local processing may include wallpaper preferences, lighting settings, notification events, and device display configurations.

Integrated third-party services may independently process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, app interactions, advertising data, and diagnostics when enabled.

4. Device Permissions

The App uses Android permissions and system access to support its features:

  • Notification Access: Supports notification-responsive lighting and may provide access to notification information.
  • Display Over Other Apps: Supports edge lighting effects over compatible screens.
  • Camera Flash: Supports flash notification effects.
  • Notifications: Allows supported notification-related functionality.
  • Wallpaper Access: Supports wallpaper customization.
  • Internet and Network Access: Supports network-enabled third-party services.
  • Background Services: Helps maintain supported lighting and notification effects.
  • Battery Optimization Settings: May support continued operation of selected background features.

Users can manage applicable permissions through Android settings.

5. Notification Privacy

Notification-responsive features may require Android Notification Listener access.

When enabled, Android may provide notification information, including notification content, to the App.

This information is handled locally for the App's notification-related functionality and is not uploaded to servers operated by FT PVT. LIMITED.

Users can disable Notification Access through their device settings at any time.

6. Local Data Storage

The App stores or processes core personalization settings locally on the user's device.

This may include selected wallpapers, edge lighting preferences, visual effects, and other application settings.

Clearing application data or uninstalling the App may remove locally stored information, subject to Android storage behavior.

Information processed separately by third-party providers is subject to their own retention policies.
