FT PVT. LIMITED respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Edge Glow & Flash Alerts handles information and permissions required for its core functionality.

1. Personal Information

Edge Glow & Flash Alerts does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

The app does not use a detected external backend, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or tracking SDK to collect or transmit user information.

2. Notification Access

Edge Glow & Flash Alerts uses Android's Notification Listener functionality to detect notification events on your device.

Notification access is used so the app can display configured visual alerts, edge lighting, glow effects, or related notification effects when a notification is received.

Depending on the information made available by Android, the app may locally access notification-related information such as:

  • The application that generated a notification
  • Notification status or events
  • Notification metadata required for the feature

This information is processed locally on your device for the app's notification-related functionality.

Notification access is controlled by Android and can be enabled or disabled by you through your device settings.

3. Display Over Other Apps

Edge Glow & Flash Alerts may request permission to display content over other applications.

This permission is used to show edge lighting, glow effects, or related visual alert elements while you are using other apps.

You can enable or disable this permission through Android system settings.

4. App Notifications

On supported Android versions, Edge Glow & Flash Alerts may request notification permission so the app can display its own notifications where required for its functionality.

You can allow or deny this permission through Android settings.

5. Battery Optimization

The app may ask you to allow it to operate without certain Android battery optimization restrictions.

This may be necessary to help Edge Glow & Flash Alerts continue detecting notification events and displaying configured visual effects while running in the background.

You control this setting through your device's Android settings.

6. Device Restart

Edge Glow & Flash Alerts may automatically restore applicable app functionality after your device restarts.

This helps maintain your previously selected app settings and notification-related functionality.

7. Local App Settings

The app may store preferences locally on your device, including settings such as:

  • Edge glow enable or disable status
  • Glow styles
  • Colors and visual preferences
  • Flash-related preferences
  • Lock-screen related preferences
  • Per-app notification preferences
  • Other application configuration settings

These settings are used only to provide and remember your selected app experience.

8. Data Sharing

Edge Glow & Flash Alerts does not use a detected advertising, analytics, attribution, or tracking service for sharing user information.

Notification-related information and application preferences used by the detected functionality are processed locally on the device.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

App settings and preferences may remain stored locally on your device while Edge Glow & Flash Alerts is installed.

You can delete locally stored app data by:

  • Clearing Edge Glow & Flash Alerts data from Android Settings, or
  • Uninstalling the application.

10. Security

We aim to limit access to information only to what is required for Edge Glow & Flash Alerts functionality.

Because notification-related processing and application preferences are handled locally for the detected version of the app, users remain in control of relevant Android permissions through their device settings.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the functionality of Edge Glow & Flash Alerts changes.

Any updated version will include a revised effective date.
