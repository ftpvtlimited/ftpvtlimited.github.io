1. Developer and Scope

FT PVT LIMITED develops Emoji Battery, an Android application providing decorative battery icons, status-bar customization, stickers, animated pets, saved styles, application-specific visibility settings, and gesture shortcuts.

This Privacy Policy explains information handling associated with these features, advertisements, integrated services, notifications, and support.

Package Name: com.pawbar.emojibattery
Website: https://ftpvtlimited.github.io/
Privacy Contact: kcorporation70@gmail.com

Some features use locally stored information. Integrated Google services may process information online. Local customization does not mean that the entire application operates offline.

2. Customization Settings and Local Records

Emoji Battery processes settings needed to remember and apply your preferences, including:

  • Selected battery icons, emoji styles, stickers, and backgrounds.
  • Colors, sizes, positions, margins, and clock formatting.
  • Pet selections, movement preferences, and related settings.
  • Enabled or disabled customization features.
  • Favourite and recently used styles.
  • Applications selected for shortcuts or hiding rules.
  • Language and onboarding preferences.
  • Certain application-state information, such as launch counts and rating-prompt status.

These records use application-local storage, including Android DataStore.

No developer-operated synchronization of the complete customization settings was identified in the tested version. Information handled by advertising and other integrated services is separate from these local settings.

3. Battery and Device Information

The application reads battery level and charging status supplied by Android to support its display features.

Decorative or fake-percentage modes are visual effects. They should not be relied on as measurements of actual battery capacity, health, or charge.

Integrated services may additionally process application and device information, installation identifiers, connectivity information, and diagnostics according to their configuration.

The tested version does not request Android location permission. However, online services may derive general location from IP information.

4. Accessibility Service

Certain status-bar features use an Android Accessibility Service that you enable separately through Android settings.

The service supports:

  • Displaying the decorative status-bar overlay.
  • Responding to window-state changes.
  • Reading the foreground application’s package name to apply your selected hiding rules.
  • Performing configured gesture shortcuts.

The inspected configuration disables window-content retrieval. Its event handler reads application-package metadata rather than screen text or private messages. No upload of that package metadata was identified in the inspected handler.

Depending on your choices and device support, gestures may open the notification shade or quick settings, lock the screen, launch a selected application, open an external camera application, or attempt a flashlight action.

You can disable the service through Android Accessibility settings. This policy does not replace the separate in-app disclosure and affirmative activation process.

5. Application Selection and Visibility Rules

The application queries launcher applications available through Android’s package-visibility system.

This allows you to select shortcut destinations and applications in which the battery or status-bar decoration should be hidden.

Selected application package names and hiding choices form part of your local configuration. The tested version does not request unrestricted application inventory through QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES.

Hiding the status-bar decoration in an application does not necessarily hide the separate on-screen pet.

6. Overlays and Background Operation

The pet feature requires Android’s Display over other apps access to display a decoration over supported applications.

An enabled pet can operate through a special-use foreground service. Boot, update, and scheduling components support enabled features and reminder behavior.

You can switch off relevant features or revoke special access through Android settings.

Refusing ordinary notification display permission does not necessarily stop the pet foreground service. Notification display and permission to operate a particular service are separate controls.

7. Advertising Through Google AdMob

Emoji Battery integrates Google AdMob. Supported formats include banner, native, interstitial, and app-open advertisements.

Availability depends on configuration, network conditions, and applicable privacy choices. Test advertisements were displayed in the reviewed development build.

According to Google’s documentation, its advertising SDK processes information such as:

  • IP addresses and general location derived from them.
  • Device or advertising identifiers.
  • Advertisement views and interactions.
  • Application interactions.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.

Google describes advertising delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention among the purposes of this processing.

Production advertising settings may differ from the tested development configuration.

Official information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Data-Disclosure Guidance

8. Advertising Privacy Choices

The application integrates Google’s User Messaging Platform to obtain advertising-consent status and display applicable privacy forms.

An Ad privacy options entry point is configured to appear when required by the integration.

Available forms and choices depend on region, provider configuration, and applicable requirements. In the test session, the provider response did not require a consent form; its acceptance and refusal flows were therefore not verified.

Advertising choices do not automatically establish that Analytics, messaging registration, performance monitoring, or remote configuration stop processing. Those services must be assessed according to their actual controls.

Official information:

  • Google User Messaging Platform
  • Google Privacy Policy

9. Google Analytics for Firebase

Firebase Analytics initializes when Firebase is available. The implementation includes event-logging support and a premium-status user-property path.

Depending on configuration, Analytics may process:

  • App-instance identifiers.
  • Application and device information.
  • Application lifecycle events and sessions.
  • Interactions included by the implementation.
  • General location derived from IP information.
  • Advertising-related information where enabled.

The inclusion of Analytics does not establish that every local customization value is transmitted.

The Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information below apply to the Firebase services discussed in this policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Analytics Data-Disclosure Guidance

10. Crash and Performance Diagnostics

Firebase Crashlytics is included for crash and error reporting. When enabled, it may process stack traces, device and application metadata, installation identifiers, logs, and configured diagnostic details.

Automatic Crashlytics collection is disabled in the tested debug build. A different release must be assessed separately.

Firebase Performance Monitoring is also included, and its SDK-enabled setting was present in the test installation. It may process startup measurements, rendering measurements, and network-request metadata according to configuration.

Diagnostic payloads were not decrypted during testing. Crashlytics being disabled does not mean that Performance Monitoring or Analytics is disabled.

Official information:

  • Firebase SDK Data-Disclosure Guidance
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

11. Remote Configuration and Installation Information

Firebase Remote Config obtains feature and advertising settings. Local fallback values are also included. A successful remote configuration fetch was observed during testing.

Firebase services may process installation identifiers and related application or device information. Applicable Installations, Sessions, and transport components form part of this processing.

A cloud-storage configuration value does not, by itself, establish that personal files or the complete local customization settings are uploaded.

Official information:

  • Firebase Remote Config Documentation
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

12. Notifications and Messaging

The application supports Firebase Cloud Messaging registration and message handling. It attempts subscription to a general audience topic.

Registration and topic synchronization failed in the test session, so successful push delivery was not verified.

Messaging may involve registration tokens and installation information. Messages can contain notification text and a destination within the application.

The app also includes local re-engagement reminders.

You can manage notification display through Android settings. Denying notification display permission does not necessarily stop registration attempts or other SDK processing.

The pet foreground-service notification serves a separate operational purpose.

Official information:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging Documentation
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

13. Premium Features and Google Play Billing

The APK includes subscription, purchase-acknowledgment, and entitlement-restoration code. Its premium feature flag is disabled in the tested build; no purchase or restoration was verified.

If enabled in a release, Google Play processes transactions. The application can receive product, offer, purchase-token, purchase-state, and entitlement information needed to provide eligible features.

Local premium entitlement is stored separately from ordinary customization preferences.

Clearing application data or uninstalling does not automatically cancel subscriptions. Manage active subscriptions through Google Play.

Official information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing Information

14. Storage, Backups, Retention, and Deletion

Local settings remain until changed or removed through available controls, Android’s Clear Storage/Clear Data option, or uninstallation.

Clearing storage removed the test application’s local DataStore records and restored first-run onboarding on relaunch. Application and SDK state was subsequently recreated.

Backup rules permit selected SharedPreferences in cloud backup and device transfer while excluding the entitlement file. This does not establish that every DataStore setting is included. Device and backup-provider behavior may vary.

Local deletion does not erase earlier advertising, analytics, messaging, performance, backup, support, or purchase records. It does not guarantee forensic erasure or subscription cancellation.

Retention and deletion of provider records depend on the applicable service settings and processes. Contact us concerning information under our control.

15. Support and External Services

The application provides feedback options and prepares email to kcorporation70@gmail.com.

Feedback can include selected issue categories, your message, and application, Android, or device details. Sending is completed through your selected email application.

If you send a message, we receive the sender address, contents, and attachments. Avoid including passwords, private messages, payment credentials, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Questions concerning support-message retention or deletion can be directed to the same address.

External websites, advertisements, applications, and integrated services operate under their own practices. Provider processing may involve locations outside your country; specific processing locations were not established in this review.

16. Children’s Privacy

Emoji and animated pets may appeal to younger users.

This policy does not establish a verified age-screening mechanism, parental-consent system, or child-directed advertising configuration.

Parents or guardians can contact us about privacy concerns. Intended audience and age-related handling must match actual application design, marketing, SDK configuration, and Play Console declarations.

A release directed to children requires an appropriate assessment of its advertisements, identifiers, services, and safeguards before deployment.

17. Security, Controls, and Privacy Requests

Android storage and permission controls help limit access, but no system provides an absolute security guarantee.

The tested APK is a debuggable development build, not evidence of a production-security assessment. No universal encryption-at-rest or all-traffic security guarantee is made.

You can:

  • Disable Accessibility or overlay access.
  • Manage notifications.
  • Review available advertising privacy options.
  • Remove supported saved items.
  • Clear local application storage or uninstall.
  • Manage subscriptions through Google Play.
  • Contact us about information provided directly.

These actions do not automatically delete remote records.

Depending on applicable law, rights may include access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, consent withdrawal, or complaints. Contact us to raise a request. Local records and identifier-based provider records may require different handling; do not send unnecessary identity documents.

18. Changes and Contact

This policy must be updated when enabled services, features, or information-handling practices change.

Its effective month and year identify the applicable version. A production release with different advertising, crash collection, purchases, audience treatment, or consent behavior requires matching disclosures.
