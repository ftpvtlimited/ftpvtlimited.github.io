1. Introduction

FT PVT. LIMITED ("we," "us," or "our") respects the privacy of users of GradeBeam ("the App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, processed, stored, and shared when users interact with the App, including information processed by integrated third-party services.

GradeBeam is a calculation utility application designed to help users perform calculations, manage measurements, and use related calculation features.

2. Information We Process

GradeBeam processes information necessary to provide its calculation functionality, including:

  • Values and measurements entered by users.
  • Calculation results.
  • Unit preferences and application settings.
  • Information required to operate application features.

Core calculation information is processed locally on the user's device. FT PVT. LIMITED does not require calculation inputs to be uploaded to company-operated servers to perform these calculations.

The App also integrates Firebase Analytics and related Google services, which may process application usage information, device-related information, and technical identifiers.

3. Local Data Processing and Storage

GradeBeam uses on-device storage mechanisms to support application preferences, calculations, and related features.

Depending on the functionality used, information may be stored using local application databases, preferences, or other Android storage mechanisms.

Locally stored information may remain on the device until it is modified, cleared, or removed through the App or applicable device controls.

Local processing does not prevent integrated third-party analytics services from processing their own technical and usage-related information.

4. Application Permissions

GradeBeam uses Android permissions and supporting capabilities necessary for application operation and integrated services.

These include:

Internet Access: Enables internet connectivity for integrated services and supported network operations.

Network State: Allows the App to check network connectivity.

Wake Lock: Supports operations that may require the device to remain active.

Advertising Identifier and Attribution Permissions: Enable supported identification and attribution capabilities used by compatible Android and Google components. The existence of these permissions does not necessarily mean advertising identifiers are actively accessed.

Internal Application Permissions: Support communication and protection of certain internal Android components.

The inspected App does not declare permissions to access the camera, microphone, precise device location, contacts, or SMS messages.

5. Firebase Analytics

GradeBeam integrates Google Analytics for Firebase, a third-party analytics service provided by Google.

Firebase Analytics supports measurement of application usage and interactions.

Depending on its configuration and operation, Firebase Analytics may process:

  • Application screen views.
  • Feature usage and interaction events.
  • Button interactions.
  • Application and device information.
  • App-instance or installation-related identifiers.
  • Technical information associated with analytics events.

The application includes analytics-related event logging functionality.

When Firebase Analytics data collection is enabled, relevant analytics information may be transmitted to Google's systems for processing and reporting.

This information is handled under Google's applicable privacy policies and service terms.

For more information:

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Firebase Privacy and Security:
https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/

6. Firebase Installations and Google Play Services

GradeBeam also includes Firebase Installations and Google Play Services components.

Firebase Installations supports installation-related identification and technical functionality required by compatible Firebase services.

Google Play Services provides supporting Android platform integrations and technical services.

Depending on the functions used, these components may process technical information and identifiers necessary for their operation.

For more information, visit:

https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/

https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. How Information Is Used

Information processed through GradeBeam and its integrated services may be used to:

  • Perform calculations requested by users.
  • Provide application features and functionality.
  • Store user preferences and application settings locally.
  • Measure application usage and feature interactions.
  • Understand application performance and usability.
  • Support ongoing maintenance and improvements.

The purposes and categories of information processed by third-party services depend on the services' actual configuration and operation.

8. Data Sharing and Third-Party Processing

GradeBeam's core calculation functionality processes calculation inputs on the user's device.

FT PVT. LIMITED does not require users to submit calculation values to its company-operated servers for these core functions.

However, the App includes Firebase Analytics and related Google components.

When enabled and operating, these services may transmit relevant analytics, device, installation, and technical information to Google.

Such processing is subject to Google's applicable privacy terms and the service configuration.

We do not represent the App as entirely free from third-party data processing.

9. Advertising and Attribution

The App includes Android advertising identifier and attribution-related permissions or components.

These capabilities may support advertising measurement or attribution functions where applicable.

Their presence does not establish that the App displays advertisements, uses personalized advertising, or actively collects advertising identifiers.

Any actual advertising or attribution-related information processing depends on the services enabled in the application.

10. Data Retention

Information stored locally by GradeBeam may remain on the user's device while the App is installed.

Users may be able to remove locally stored application information by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.

Information processed by Google Analytics for Firebase or other applicable Google services may be retained according to their configured retention settings and applicable service terms.

Removing the App does not necessarily remove information already transmitted to a third-party service.

11. Data Security

FT PVT. LIMITED recognizes the importance of protecting information processed through GradeBeam.

The App uses Android application functionality and local storage mechanisms to support its features.

Google and other applicable third-party service providers maintain their own technical and organizational measures for information processed through their systems.

Although privacy and security are important considerations, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Children's Privacy

GradeBeam is a general-purpose calculation and utility application and is not specifically designed or presented as a children's application.

Its integrated services may process technical or usage information as described in this Privacy Policy.

Parents or legal guardians who have concerns about information relating to a child may contact us using the details below.

Where applicable, relevant children's privacy laws and Google Play requirements govern the processing of children's information.

13. User Choices and Privacy Rights

Users can manage certain information and device-level controls through Android settings.

Depending on the device and available functionality, users may:

  • Review or manage applicable Android permissions.
  • Clear locally stored application data.
  • Uninstall the App.
  • Review applicable Google privacy settings.
  • Contact FT PVT. LIMITED with privacy-related questions or requests.

Depending on the user's location, applicable privacy laws may provide additional rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to certain personal data processing.

Requests concerning information processed by FT PVT. LIMITED can be submitted using the contact email below.

14. International Data Processing

Information processed by Google services may be handled using infrastructure located in different countries.

Google's applicable privacy notices and service terms provide additional information about its processing practices and international data transfers.
