1. About Harfoona and This Policy

Harfoona provides learning activities involving letters, sounds, tracing, and play, including Arabic and Urdu learning content. Its features allow learners to explore activities while parents or guardians help manage profiles and settings.

The current reviewed version operates with local learner profiles and locally stored progress. It does not require an online account or provide a developer-operated cloud synchronization service.

This policy distinguishes between:

Information processed locally by the current application.

Information you choose to provide directly to FT PVT LIMITED.

Device services used for functions such as speech output.

Advertising, analytics, and crash-reporting services planned for future versions.

Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics were not included in the reviewed APK. Their planned roles are explained in Section 10.

2. Information Used by the Application

Depending on the features used, Harfoona processes the following information.

Learner Profiles

A local learner profile can include:

An optional nickname.

A selected avatar.

Information connecting the profile to its learning progress.

The currently selected learner profile.

These profiles help separate the learning experiences of people sharing a device. They are not online accounts and do not require an email address, telephone number, or password in the reviewed version.

Parents and guardians should use a nickname rather than a child’s full name. Please avoid entering addresses, school details, contact information, or other unnecessary personal information into a nickname.

Learning Activities and Progress

Harfoona processes information about the learning activities used within a profile, including selected content and saved progress.

This supports functions such as continuing a learning experience and displaying progress associated with the relevant profile.

Locally recorded progress should not be confused with information being sent to an analytics provider. The current reviewed version does not include an analytics SDK.

Application Preferences

The application also processes settings needed to provide the selected experience, such as language, audio, voice, and other available application preferences.

3. How Information Is Obtained

Information is obtained through:

Details entered or selected when creating or editing a local learner profile.

Interactions with learning activities.

Choices made in application or parent settings.

Responses from Android services used for local audio and speech functions.

Messages and attachments you voluntarily send to our support email.

The reviewed version does not obtain learner information through an online registration form or a developer-operated account system.

4. Purposes of Local Processing

Harfoona uses locally processed information to:

Create and distinguish learner profiles.

Associate progress with the correct learner.

Display selected learning content.

Continue learning activities.

Remember relevant application preferences.

Play educational sounds and spoken content.

Support profile management and deletion.

The current reviewed version does not upload these local records to a server operated by FT PVT LIMITED.

5. Local Storage and Cloud Functionality

Harfoona uses a local database within the application’s storage to retain application state, learner profiles, and progress.

The reviewed version does not provide developer-operated cloud synchronization or cloud recovery. A profile created on one installation is not an online account that can be used to retrieve progress from our servers on another device.

The application’s normal Android backup configuration is disabled. Device manufacturers, operating-system services, manual copying, and device-management tools may have their own behavior outside the application’s direct control.

Anyone with access to the application on an unlocked device may be able to view or modify locally stored profiles and progress. Parents and guardians should manage access to shared devices accordingly.

6. Audio Playback

Harfoona includes audio files used for educational sounds and related playback.

Playing an included audio file is an output function. It does not require the learner to speak or provide a voice recording.

The current reviewed application does not request microphone permission and does not include a learner voice-recording or voice-scoring feature.

Listening activities should therefore not be understood as recording the learner’s pronunciation or sending the learner’s voice to FT PVT LIMITED.

7. Text-to-Speech and Installed Voice Services

Some speech functions use Android’s text-to-speech system.

For these functions, Harfoona passes the text to be spoken to the installed speech engine. The reviewed implementation selects voices identified by Android as not requiring a network connection.

Available languages and voices depend on the device, installed speech engine, and downloaded voice resources. Some speech functions may be unavailable if a suitable offline voice is not installed.

The speech engine is a separate device service. Its provider may vary between devices and may have its own privacy practices. Harfoona’s use of an offline-designated voice does not establish every independent practice of the installed engine.

You can review the installed speech engine and its settings through your device’s text-to-speech settings.

8. Permissions and Device Access

The current reviewed application does not declare Android permissions for:

Internet access.

Microphone recording.

Camera access.

Approximate or precise location.

Contacts or calendar access.

Photos or shared media access.

The application includes an internal component-protection permission. This is not a request to read a learner’s personal information.

Android can provide local storage, audio playback, and communication with an installed speech engine without these dangerous permissions.

The absence of Internet permission in Harfoona does not determine the independent behavior of other applications or operating-system services.

9. Retention and Deletion of Local Information

Locally saved profiles, preferences, and progress remain in application storage until removed through the relevant application controls, Android’s application-data controls, or uninstallation.

Deleting a Learner Profile

Harfoona includes an option to delete a learner profile and its associated progress. Use this option when an individual learner’s records are no longer needed.

Deleting All Application Data

The application includes an option to delete all application data on the device. Android’s application settings also provide controls for clearing application storage.

Uninstalling the Application

Uninstalling generally removes the application’s private storage. Because the reviewed version does not provide developer-operated cloud recovery, local records should not be assumed to remain recoverable after deletion or uninstallation.

Deletion is not a promise of irreversible forensic erasure. It also does not remove information independently retained outside the application, including an email previously sent to support.

10. Planned Advertising, Analytics, and Crash Reporting

FT PVT LIMITED plans to add Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics.

These services are not included in the current reviewed APK. The following descriptions explain their intended roles and documented capabilities; they do not state that the services currently receive information from this version.

Before these integrations are released, their actual data handling must be reviewed and this policy updated accordingly.

Provider: Google

Planned purpose: Displaying advertisements.

Depending on the SDK version, configuration, age-related treatment, and applicable privacy choices, Google’s advertising SDK may process:

Device or advertising identifiers.

Advertisement views and interactions.

Diagnostic information.

Google documents advertising delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention among the purposes of this processing.

This description does not establish that every listed category will be collected by a future Harfoona release. The final advertising implementation must determine the applicable collection and disclosures.

Because Harfoona is child-focused, advertising requires particular attention to age-related configuration, permitted identifiers, advertisement suitability, and applicable Google Play Families requirements.

The availability of advertising, analytics, and diagnostic choices depends on the released implementation and applicable requirements.

11. Other Software Components

Harfoona uses Flutter, Android components, and a SQLite database integration to provide its interface, storage, and device functionality.

A software library being included in the application does not mean that its publisher receives learner information.

The installed text-to-speech engine is a separate service. Because its provider differs between devices, no single speech-provider privacy policy applies to every installation.

12. Children’s Privacy and Parent Management

Harfoona’s activities are designed for young learners with assistance from a parent or guardian.

In the current reviewed version:

Learner profiles are local rather than online accounts.

A nickname is optional.

Profiles and progress are stored on the device.

No microphone recording feature is included.

No advertising, analytics, or remote crash-reporting SDK is included.

The application provides a parent area for managing relevant settings and learner information. Access to that area should not be interpreted as identity verification or verified parental consent for online processing.

Adding advertising or other online services changes the children’s privacy assessment. Appropriate age-related settings, legal requirements, disclosures, and any required consent must be assessed before such an update is released.

Parents and guardians can use the local deletion controls or contact FT PVT LIMITED about information provided directly to us.

13. Support Communications

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or other information you choose to send.

Please describe the issue without including unnecessary information about a child. Avoid sending a child’s full name, photograph, voice recording, location, or other sensitive details unless necessary to explain the request.

Support messages are separate from locally stored application records. Deleting a learner profile or uninstalling Harfoona does not delete an email already sent.

For questions about support-message retention or deletion, contact kcorporation70@gmail.com.

14. Information Sharing and External Services

The current reviewed application does not include a developer-operated feature for uploading learner profiles or progress.

Information may nevertheless reach another service when:

You send an email through your chosen email provider.

The application passes text to an installed speech engine.

You independently provide information to an external website or application.

Those services handle information under their own practices.

If the planned Google services are enabled in a future release, their actual processing and recipients must be reflected in an updated policy.

15. Security

Harfoona uses Android application storage and access controls for locally stored information.

No method of electronic storage or processing provides an absolute security guarantee. Protect access to the device, particularly when it is shared.

This policy does not promise application-level encryption of every local record, guaranteed recovery after deletion, or protection against every form of device compromise.

16. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

Parents and users can:

Use a nickname instead of a real name.

Manage learner profiles and available preferences.

Delete a learner profile and its progress.

Delete locally stored application data.

Manage available audio and speech settings.

Use Android’s application-data and uninstall controls.

Contact FT PVT LIMITED about information supplied directly to us.

Local controls affect information stored by that installation. They do not automatically remove information retained by an email provider, speech-engine provider, or another external service.

Depending on applicable law and the processing concerned, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, objection, or other privacy matters. You can contact us to raise a request.

Please do not send identity documents or a child’s personal records unless they are specifically needed to address the request.

17. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application features, third-party integrations, or information-handling practices change.

The effective month and year will identify the applicable policy version. An update enabling online services must accurately describe the resulting practices.
