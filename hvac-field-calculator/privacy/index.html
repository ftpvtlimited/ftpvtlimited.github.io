1. About this policy

FT PVT. LIMITED ("we", "us" or "our") provides HVAC Field Calculator (the "App"). This policy explains information processing associated with the App, including calculations, saved results, settings, analytics, diagnostics, notifications and remote configuration.

Our website is https://ftpvtlimited.github.io/. For App support or privacy questions, contact kcorporation70@gmail.com. Browsing our website is also subject to the website's separate privacy notice.

2. Calculations, settings and saved results

The App processes measurements and selections you enter to perform HVAC calculations. Depending on the tool, these include pressures, temperatures, airflow, dimensions, electrical measurements, refrigerant selections, units and configured altitude.

When you save a result, the App stores calculation-history information locally on your device. A saved record can include the tool name, result summary, assessment, refrigerant, altitude and saving time. The App also uses local storage for preferences and remembered settings. The configured history limit determines how many recent saved records are kept; older records may be removed when that limit is reached.

Local calculation history and Google/Firebase processing are separate. Tool-use events, settings or diagnostic context associated with calculations can be processed by the services described below. A local saved-history feature should not be understood as meaning that all information associated with App use remains on the device.

3. Usage information and technical identifiers

The App uses Google Firebase services for analytics, crash reporting, performance monitoring, notifications and remote configuration. These services process information according to their role, configuration and operation.

  • App activity: app opens, screens viewed, tools opened or completed, interactions, settings changes, job actions, errors and timing information.
  • Calculation-related context: tool and refrigerant selections, unit system, configured altitude or altitude grouping, calculation method and result-status information, where included in events or diagnostics.
  • Technical information: App version, operating-system information, device characteristics, crash information, performance measurements and network-request metadata.
  • Identifiers: Firebase installation identifiers, analytics app-instance identifiers, crash-reporting identifiers and messaging registration tokens used by the relevant services. These identifiers are not the same as an App account, and they should not be assumed to be anonymous simply because a name is not requested.

Online service requests also expose connection information, such as an IP address, to the receiving service. This is different from the App requesting access to your device's precise location. The calculator does not require a login account, contacts, camera, microphone or precise-location permission for its calculation features.

4. Google and Firebase services

Google Analytics for Firebase

We use Analytics to understand App usage, feature interactions and errors, and to inform improvements. Events and user properties can include the tool, screen, refrigerant, unit system, altitude grouping and other usage context described above, together with service-generated technical information and identifiers. Analytics processing depends on the settings applied to the service.

Firebase Crashlytics

We use Crashlytics to investigate crashes and non-fatal errors. Reports can include stack traces, relevant App and device state, version information, installation identifiers, diagnostic logs and custom context such as the current tool, screen, units, refrigerant or configured altitude.

Firebase Performance Monitoring

We use Performance Monitoring to understand App responsiveness and technical performance. Depending on the trace or request being monitored, information can include timing measurements, App and device characteristics, installation identifiers, and network-request information such as request URLs, response codes, duration and transfer sizes.

Firebase Cloud Messaging

We use Cloud Messaging to support push notifications. It uses installation identifiers and registration tokens to address App installations. The App includes a general notification-topic subscription, and its notification-handling code can record notification interactions. Android notification settings control whether notifications may be displayed; disabling display permission does not by itself disable every Firebase service or all installation processing.

Firebase Remote Config

We use Remote Config to retrieve App configuration, including feature controls, update-related settings, history limits and informational content. Remote Config uses technical and installation information to supply configuration to App installations. Configuration values may change without installing a new App version.

Firebase Installations and supporting services

Firebase Installations creates and manages identifiers and authentication tokens used by Firebase services. Supporting session and data-transport components help the relevant services operate. Their information processing depends on which services are enabled and their settings.

Google is the provider of these services. Read the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security information. The latter explains service-specific processing and retention. Further Analytics information is available in Google Analytics privacy information.

5. How information is used

The processing described in this policy supports calculations, remembered preferences, saved results, remote configuration, notification delivery, usage analysis, reliability monitoring, troubleshooting and support. Information supplied in a support request is used to handle that request.

Analytics and diagnostic information is separate from the calculator's engineering result. It must not be interpreted as an App account or as evidence that a user's real-world identity has been verified.

6. Permissions and device capabilities

The App uses internet access and network-state information for its online services. It includes messaging and background-work capabilities, including wake-lock support. On Android versions that require notification permission, notification display is subject to that permission and your system settings.

The Android build also includes installation-referrer and advertising-attribution support associated with its Google libraries. These capabilities are distinct from showing advertisements, and a declared permission does not mean that every possible identifier is accessed. Identifier availability depends on Android restrictions, device services and configuration.

7. Information you provide when contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, any name you include and attachments you choose to provide. This information is handled separately from local calculation history. Our published contact address uses Gmail; Google and your own email provider also process correspondence under their respective privacy practices.

The App does not provide a separate email-retention or email-deletion control. Contact the privacy email below with a question or request concerning support correspondence.

8. Recipients and third-party processing

Google/Firebase receives and processes information needed for the services described in this policy. Google processes relevant customer and service information under the applicable service terms and privacy documentation. Google can also process information associated with support email through Gmail.

Local history is not the same as cloud synchronization. The App does not provide a user-account-based cloud-history feature in the functionality covered by this policy. Visiting an external website or contacting another provider involves that provider's own information-handling practices.

9. Your choices and local data controls

  • Use the Jobs controls to delete individual saved entries or clear saved history.
  • Use Settings to change available preferences or forget remembered values. Forgetting remembered values is separate from deleting saved jobs.
  • Use Android settings to manage notification permissions and other available system controls.
  • Android's app-storage controls can clear the App's local data. Uninstalling the App ordinarily removes its private local data, subject to device backup and restore behaviour.
  • Contact us about information access, deletion, objections or other privacy requests.

Deleting local history, clearing App storage or uninstalling the App does not automatically delete information already processed by Google/Firebase or correspondence in a support mailbox. The App does not currently offer an in-app Analytics or diagnostics opt-out switch. This privacy notice is not a substitute for a separate consent request where applicable law requires one.

10. Retention and deletion

Local saved calculations remain subject to user deletion, App-data removal and the configured history limit. There is no single fixed retention period covering every type of information described in this policy.

Analytics, diagnostics, installation and notification-related records follow the settings and service-specific retention and deletion rules applicable to the relevant Google/Firebase service. These rules differ between services and are described in Firebase's privacy information and Google Analytics retention guidance. A successful local deletion does not confirm deletion of these separate service records.

Contact us to request information about the retention or deletion applicable to your data. Requests concerning support correspondence or service-held information are separate from deleting saved calculations in the App. Legal rights and any applicable exceptions depend on the circumstances and governing law.

11. Storage and security

Saved calculations are stored in the App's local database and preferences. Android's application sandbox normally restricts access to private App storage. The local history database does not provide a separate App-level database-encryption layer; device encryption, backup and debugging protections are separate Android/device features.

Google describes HTTPS transport and other safeguards for Firebase in its security documentation. This does not mean that every device, local file or support message has the same protection. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

12. International processing

Google/Firebase and email services may process information in countries other than your own. Applicable processing arrangements, locations, retention rules and safeguards depend on the service and configuration. Further information is available in Google's privacy documentation and the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms.

13. Privacy requests and regional rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or a copy of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Where processing lawfully relies on consent, applicable law may also provide a right to withdraw that consent. These rights are subject to their legal conditions and exceptions.

Send requests to kcorporation70@gmail.com, identifying HVAC Field Calculator and explaining the request. Some service information is associated with installation identifiers rather than your email address, which may affect how relevant records can be identified. You may also have a right to contact your local data-protection authority.

14. Children's privacy

HVAC Field Calculator is intended for HVAC technicians, engineers and people learning HVAC work. It is not designed as a child-directed service. The App does not provide an age-verification or parental-consent mechanism.

If you are a parent or guardian concerned about information relating to a child, contact kcorporation70@gmail.com so the concern can be reviewed. Children's privacy requirements depend on the child's location, age and the processing involved; a store content rating should not be treated as a parental-consent mechanism.

15. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's functionality, services or information processing changes. An updated policy will identify its effective month and year. Any additional notice or consent required by applicable law is separate from displaying an updated policy.
