1. About This Privacy Policy

FT PVT. LIMITED (“we,” “us,” or “our”) publishes Track your Drive - GPS Tracker (“the App”). This Privacy Policy explains how the App and the third-party services it uses access, collect, use, store, and disclose information. It also explains how to manage permissions, make advertising privacy choices, and submit a privacy request.

2. Information We Process

The information processed depends on the features you use, your Android permissions, your privacy choices, your region, and the services enabled in the App.

  • Location and movement information: Approximate or precise location, latitude and longitude, location accuracy, timestamps, and movement information used by GPS features.
  • Device and connection information: Device model, Android version, App version, IP address, and network information.
  • App activity and diagnostics: App events, session information, crash reports, and performance data where the relevant service is enabled.
  • Identifiers and privacy preferences: Installation identifiers, notification tokens, advertising identifiers where available, and consent or opt-out selections.
  • Purchase information: Product identifiers, purchase tokens, and transaction or subscription status, where purchases are offered.
  • Settings and support information: Preferences saved on your device and the information you choose to provide when contacting us.

3. GPS Tracking and Location Permissions

The App requests Android location permission to provide GPS tracking, show your current position, and support location-based trip and movement features. When a feature uses location, the App may process a series of location readings and timestamps to determine changes in position or calculate travel distance.

Depending on your device and Android version, you can grant approximate or precise location access. You can change or revoke that access in Android settings. Limiting location permission may reduce tracking accuracy or make some features unavailable. To remove information already stored, see Section 18.

4. Maps, Place Search, and Mapbox

The App uses Mapbox services to support map features. Depending on the feature you use, a request may load a map, search for a place, or convert an address to coordinates or coordinates to an address.

Mapbox may receive technical information such as your IP address and the data needed to complete the request. For example, a place search may send the text you enter, and a location-enabled request may send coordinates. See the Mapbox Privacy Policy.

5. Information Stored on Your Device

The App may save preferences, settings, and feature state locally to remember your choices and support normal operation. Locally stored information may remain until you remove it through an available App control, clear the App’s storage in Android settings, uninstall the App, or the operating system removes it.

Clearing App storage removes local App data and may reset your preferences. Information held by us or an online service is handled through the retention and deletion processes described in Sections 17 and 18.

6. Internet and Network Access

The App uses internet access for online features such as maps, advertising, consent messages, Firebase services, and Google Play purchases. When your device connects to one of these services, the service may receive your IP address and technical information needed to process the request. The App may also check network availability before using an online feature.

7. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and maintain GPS and map features, remember App preferences, apply privacy choices, deliver notifications, and update App configuration. We also use information to diagnose crashes, improve reliability, deliver and measure ads, help detect fraud or invalid ad traffic, verify purchases where offered, respond to support and privacy requests, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

8. Firebase Crashlytics and Diagnostics

The App may use Firebase Crashlytics to identify errors and improve stability. Crash reports may include the time of a crash, a stack trace, App version, Android version, device model, device state, and installation or session identifiers.

Where analytics or performance monitoring is enabled, the relevant service may process App events, session details, or performance measurements such as load times and errors. See Firebase Privacy and Security and the Firebase Crashlytics documentation.

9. Firebase Cloud Messaging and Notifications

The App may display local notifications and receive push notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging. Firebase Cloud Messaging may process an installation identifier, registration token, and other technical information needed to route messages to your App installation.

You can manage whether the App displays notifications in Android settings. See the Firebase Cloud Messaging documentation.

10. Firebase Remote Config

The App may use Firebase Remote Config to update configuration values or change feature behavior without requiring a new App version. Firebase Remote Config may use an installation identifier and technical information to deliver the applicable settings to your App installation. See the Firebase Remote Config documentation.

11. Advertising and Ad Mediation

The App displays ads through Google AdMob Network and third-party advertising networks. Ad mediation allows multiple networks to supply ads. The networks involved in a particular ad request can vary by region, ad availability, App configuration, and privacy choices.

Depending on the network and applicable settings, an advertising provider may receive information directly from your device. This may include your IP address, approximate region inferred from your IP address, device and App information, an Android advertising identifier where available, consent signals, and ad interaction data such as impressions, clicks, and video completions.

Advertising providers may use this information to select and deliver ads, limit repeated ads, measure campaign performance, prevent invalid traffic, and operate their advertising services. Personalized ads may be shown where permitted by applicable law and your privacy choices. Contextual, non-personalized, and limited ads may still involve processing for delivery, measurement, frequency management, or fraud prevention.

  2. AppLovin — AppLovin Privacy Policy

13. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

The App may use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display privacy notices, record your selections, and communicate applicable privacy signals for ad requests. Where the App displays a Privacy Options control, you can use it to review or update your selections.

You can also use available Android or Google account advertising controls, or the controls described in the providers’ policies in Section 12. Where processing relies on consent, withdrawing consent affects future processing based on that consent. You may continue to see ads after changing your choices. You can also email kcorporation70@gmail.com to submit an advertising privacy request. See Google’s UMP privacy guidance.

14. Google Play Purchases and Subscriptions

If the App offers paid features, subscriptions, or in-app purchases, Google Play processes the transaction. Google may process payment and account information, purchase history, and data needed to protect the transaction.

We may receive a purchase token, product identifier, and transaction or subscription status to verify purchases, restore eligible access, and provide purchased features. You can review purchases and manage subscriptions through Google Play. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

15. Support and Privacy Correspondence

When you contact us, we may receive your email address, your message, and any attachments or technical details you choose to share. We use this information to investigate your inquiry, respond to you, and keep records where reasonably necessary.

For a technical issue, the App version, Android version, and a description of the problem may help us respond. Please limit the information you send to what is needed for your request.

16. Who May Receive Information

Google and Firebase may receive information for App services and diagnostics. Mapbox may receive information for map and location requests. Google Play may receive information for purchases. The advertising partners listed in Section 12 may receive information for ad delivery, measurement, and related purposes.

A provider may receive data directly from your device when its service is used. Some providers may process information for their own purposes under their privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by law or where lawful and reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, address security incidents, or protect users.

17. Data Retention

We retain information we hold for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected. For example, we may retain correspondence to resolve a support request, purchase records to verify access or meet recordkeeping obligations, and diagnostics to investigate recurring errors.

Locally stored information is subject to the device controls described in Section 5. Third-party providers apply their own retention periods to information they hold. See Firebase Privacy and Security for details on retention by Firebase service.

18. Deleting Your Information

You can remove local App information through an available in-app deletion or reset option, by clearing the App’s storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the App.

To request deletion of information we hold outside your device, email kcorporation70@gmail.com. Include the App name and enough detail to identify the information you want deleted. We may request reasonable verification before acting. We may retain certain records where required or permitted by law, such as records needed for transactions, security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution.

If a provider holds information independently, you can also use the deletion process or privacy contact in that provider’s policy.

19. Information Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Android provides controls to manage the App’s access to location and its ability to display notifications. Online providers apply security measures to the information they process. See Firebase Privacy and Security for details about protections used by Firebase services.

20. Children’s Privacy

Children’s privacy protections vary by jurisdiction. In the United States, the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Rule (COPPA) applies to the personal information of children under 13 in circumstances covered by that rule. Where applicable law requires it, we will provide notice to a parent or legal guardian and obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting, using, or disclosing a child’s personal information.

Location data may reveal a child’s whereabouts or movements. Where children’s privacy rules apply, location processing, use of device identifiers, and advertising are subject to applicable restrictions. Where Google Play Families requirements apply, ads shown to children and users of unknown age are subject to restrictions on personalized advertising, identifiers, and eligible ad SDK versions. See the FTC’s COPPA guidance and Google Play Families Policies.

Where applicable, a parent or legal guardian may ask to review, correct, or delete a child’s personal information, withdraw consent, or request that future collection or use covered by that consent stop. We may verify the requester’s identity and authority before disclosing information or acting on a request.

If you believe the App collected a child’s personal information without required permission, email kcorporation70@gmail.com with the subject “Children’s Privacy Request.” We will review the request and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

21. International Data Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence when your device uses online features such as maps, advertising, notifications, and diagnostics. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, those transfers are subject to the relevant legal requirements and provider arrangements.

22. Your Privacy Rights

European Economic Area and United Kingdom. Where applicable, we process personal information to provide a requested service, with your consent where required, for legitimate interests such as security and troubleshooting where permitted, or to comply with a legal obligation. Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of your information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your data protection authority.

California and other U.S. states. Some advertising disclosures may qualify as a “sale,” “sharing,” or “targeted advertising” under applicable state law. Where provided by law, you may appeal a decision on your request and exercise your rights without unlawful discrimination.

To submit a privacy request or appeal, email kcorporation70@gmail.com. We may ask for reasonable verification where permitted or required. See Section 13 for advertising privacy controls.

23. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when App features, service providers, data practices, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy on the App’s Privacy Policy page and update the date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or seek consent.

24. Contact Us

For questions, complaints, parental requests, or requests about your personal information, contact us at kcorporation70@gmail.com.
