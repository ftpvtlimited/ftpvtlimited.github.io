FT PVT. LIMITED (“we,” “our,” or “us”) provides the Look Check mobile application (“Look Check,” the “App,” or the “Service”).

This Privacy Policy explains how Look Check accesses, processes, stores, uses, and shares information when you use the App.

By using Look Check, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Process

Look Check is designed to help users analyze an outfit and determine whether it may be suitable for a selected event or occasion.

Depending on the features you choose to use, the App may process:

  • Photos captured using your device camera
  • Photos selected from your device
  • Outfit images
  • Event or occasion selections
  • Saved looks or previous checks
  • App preferences and settings
  • Device and technical information
  • Purchase or subscription status
  • Advertising-related information where advertising is enabled
  • Privacy and consent choices where applicable

Look Check does not require your name, email address, phone number, contacts, SMS messages, microphone recordings, or precise location to provide its main outfit-checking functionality.

2. Photos and Image Processing

Look Check allows users to take a photo or select an existing image for outfit-related analysis.

The App uses on-device machine-learning technology for functions that may include:

  • Detecting a person in an image
  • Detecting facial landmarks required for image processing
  • Analyzing body pose
  • Person or selfie segmentation
  • Separating a person from the image background
  • Supporting outfit-related analysis

These machine-learning processes are intended to take place locally on your device.

Look Check is not intended to identify who a person is, perform identity-based facial recognition, create a remote biometric profile, or maintain a remote biometric database.

Your outfit photos are not intended to be uploaded to an external artificial-intelligence service for outfit analysis.

3. Camera and Photo Permissions

Look Check may request access to certain device permissions when required for a feature you choose to use.

Camera

Camera access is used when you choose to take an outfit photo directly through the App.

Photos and Media

Photo or media access may be used when you choose an existing image from your device.

You can deny or revoke these permissions through your Android device settings.

If you deny a required permission, the related feature may not function correctly.
