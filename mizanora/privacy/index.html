1. About Mizanora and This Policy

Mizanora provides prayer times, Qibla direction, Hijri date tools, a Tasbih counter, Zakat estimates, currency calculations, Arabic number wording, and duas.

The current reviewed version performs its utility calculations on your device. It does not require an online account or provide a developer-operated synchronization service.

This policy distinguishes between:

Information processed and stored locally by Mizanora.

Information you choose to copy, export, or share.

Information you provide directly when contacting support.

External applications and device services used for particular functions.

Advertising, analytics, and crash-reporting services planned for future versions.

Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics were not included in the reviewed APK. Their planned roles are explained in Section 11.

2. Information You Enter or Select

The information Mizanora processes depends on the features you use.

Prayer Times and Qibla

The app processes your selected city or location coordinates, together with relevant prayer calculation preferences.

These details are used to calculate prayer times, Qibla direction, and related information on your device.

Zakat Calculations

The app processes financial figures you enter, which can include:

Cash and bank balances.

Gold and silver quantities, purity, and prices.

Business assets and amounts owed to you.

Debts and other applicable deductions.

Values used to determine the Zakat threshold.

Calculated results and associated dates.

These are figures supplied by you. Mizanora does not connect to your bank account to obtain them.

Currency Calculations

The app processes currency selections, amounts, and exchange rates you enter to calculate conversions.

The reviewed version does not obtain exchange rates from an online rate service.

Dates and Age Calculations

The app processes dates selected for Gregorian/Hijri conversion, age calculations, and day counts.

Entering a birth date for a calculation is not an online registration or identity-verification process.

Tasbih and Saved Content

The app processes Tasbih selections, counts, targets, session names, saved Arabic amounts, labels, and drafts.

Some saved content may reveal personal financial circumstances or religious activity. You should therefore consider it private even though it is handled locally.

Application Preferences

Mizanora also processes settings such as language, theme, onboarding choices, and other available application preferences.

3. How Information Is Obtained

Information is obtained through:

Values you type into calculators or saved items.

Dates, cities, languages, and preferences you select.

Your interactions with the Tasbih counter and other features.

Android location services when you request location assistance and grant permission.

Device sensors used for compass functionality.

Messages and attachments you voluntarily send to support.

The current reviewed version does not obtain this information through an online account-registration system.

4. Purposes of Processing

Mizanora uses information to provide the function you select, including:

Calculating prayer times.

Determining Qibla direction and related distance information.

Converting dates and calculating age or day counts.

Counting and continuing Tasbih sessions.

Calculating and displaying Zakat estimates.

Converting currency amounts using your entered rate.

Converting numbers into Arabic words.

Saving records, drafts, and preferences.

Generating content you choose to copy, export, or share.

Locally saved utility records are not uploaded to a developer-operated synchronization service in the current reviewed version.

5. Location Access and Your Choices

Mizanora requests approximate or precise location permission to help determine prayer times and Qibla direction.

When you request location assistance, Android may provide a previously available location or obtain a new location through its available location providers. The resulting coordinates are used for local calculations.

You can decline location permission and select a city manually instead. You can also change or revoke permission through Android’s application settings.

The current reviewed version does not include a background location-tracking service or a developer-operated location-upload feature.

Android’s location services may have their own device settings and provider behavior. Mizanora’s local use of coordinates should not be confused with every independent practice of the operating system or its location providers.

6. Compass and Other Device Functions

The Qibla feature uses available device sensors to determine compass direction.

Sensor availability, calibration, nearby magnetic interference, and device conditions can affect the result. Compass readings are used to support the local Qibla feature.

Mizanora also uses Android functions for private application storage, copying text, generating PDF files, and opening sharing options.

These functions do not, by themselves, mean that their contents are sent to FT PVT LIMITED.

7. Local Storage and Saved Records

Mizanora stores application state, settings, and saved records within its private application storage on your device.

Zakat results may be saved automatically. Other saved content can include Tasbih sessions, Arabic amounts, and drafts.

The application uses local database and preference storage to support these functions. It does not provide developer-operated cloud synchronization or account-based recovery in the reviewed version.

The application’s configured backup rules exclude its data from normal Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup. Copies created through other applications, manual copying, and independent device-management tools are separate from this configuration.

Anyone who can access Mizanora on an unlocked device may be able to view locally saved records. Protect access to your device, particularly if it is shared.

8. Retention and Deletion of Local Information

Locally saved records remain in application storage until removed through the available deletion controls, reset functions, Android application-data controls, or uninstallation.

Deleting Individual Records

You can use the available controls to delete saved items.

Resetting Application Data

The Reset app data option is intended to clear locally saved application records and restore the relevant application state.

Uninstalling Mizanora

Uninstalling generally removes the application’s private storage. Because the reviewed version does not provide developer-operated cloud recovery, deleted local records should not be assumed to remain recoverable from our servers.

Limits of Local Deletion

Deleting records, resetting the app, or uninstalling does not remove:

Text copied into another application.

PDFs saved outside Mizanora.

Messages or attachments already sent.

Copies retained by another person or service.

Support emails already received by FT PVT LIMITED.

Deletion should not be understood as a guarantee of irreversible forensic erasure.

9. Copying, Sharing, and PDF Exports

Mizanora allows you to copy certain results, share text, and generate or share PDFs.

Copying Text

When you copy a result, the selected text is placed on the device clipboard. Clipboard handling depends on Android and other software with permitted access to it.

Take particular care when copying financial amounts or personal labels.

Sharing Text or Files

When you choose to share content, Android passes the selected text or file to the application or recipient you select.

That application or recipient may store, transmit, or further process the information under its own privacy practices. Mizanora cannot remove copies after they have been delivered elsewhere.

Temporary PDF Files

PDFs are generated locally in the application’s export cache.

The application includes cleanup for older export files and reset-related cleanup. Older-file cleanup runs when the relevant cleanup process occurs; it does not guarantee removal at an exact time.

Deleting a temporary export from Mizanora does not delete a copy saved or received outside the app.

10. Accounts, Connectivity, and External Actions

The current reviewed version does not require an online account and does not include a developer-operated server-sync feature.

It also does not declare the Android Internet permission.

However, this does not mean that no information can ever leave the device. You can choose actions that open another application, including a browser, email application, store application, or sharing recipient.

Those applications may use their own network connections and handle information under their own practices.

12. Other Software Components

Mizanora uses Flutter, Android components, and local storage libraries to provide its interface, calculations, persistence, and device functionality.

A software library being included in the application does not mean that its publisher receives your information.

Similarly, local compass readings, preferences, and database records should not be described as information shared with a software publisher merely because that publisher supplied a library.

13. External Links and Websites

When you choose an external link, another application may open the destination.

The destination website or service may receive ordinary connection information, such as an IP address and browser information, and may use its own storage or tracking technologies.

External services operate under their own privacy practices. Review their privacy information before submitting personal information to them.

14. Support Communications

If you email FT PVT LIMITED, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or other information you choose to send.

Please explain the issue without including unnecessary sensitive information. Avoid sending detailed financial records, precise location information, or private saved content unless necessary for your request.

Support messages are separate from locally stored application records. Deleting a saved item, resetting Mizanora, or uninstalling the application does not delete a message already sent to support.

For questions concerning support-message retention or deletion, contact kcorporation70@gmail.com.

15. Information Sharing

The current reviewed version does not include a developer-operated feature for uploading locally saved utility records.

Information may nevertheless reach another application or service when:

You share text or a PDF.

You open an external website.

You send an email.

A device service operates independently under its own settings.

If the planned Google services are enabled in a future release, the resulting processing and recipients must be described in an updated policy.

Local storage, user-directed sharing, and processing by an enabled online service are different activities and should not be treated as interchangeable.

16. Children’s Privacy

Location information, financial entries, and other user-created records can be sensitive for younger users as well as adults.

The current reviewed application does not include an online child-account system or a verified parental-consent process. This policy does not establish a minimum age or represent that a child-specific advertising configuration has been implemented.

A parent or guardian concerned about information provided directly to FT PVT LIMITED can contact us using the email address below.

Any release intended for children must have its audience settings, location access, third-party integrations, and any required safeguards assessed before publication. Adding advertising or analytics requires a renewed assessment of children’s privacy implications.

17. Security

Mizanora uses Android application storage and access controls for locally stored information.

No method of electronic storage or transmission provides an absolute security guarantee. Protect access to your device and review content before copying, exporting, or sharing it.

This policy does not promise application-level encryption of every local record, guaranteed recovery after deletion, or removal of every copy held by an external recipient.

You can:

Decline or revoke location permission.

Select a city manually.

Change available application preferences.

Delete saved records.

Reset local application data.

Decide whether to copy, export, or share information.

Use Android’s application-data and uninstall controls.

Contact FT PVT LIMITED about information supplied directly to us.

Depending on applicable law and the processing concerned, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, objection, or other privacy matters. Contact us to raise a request.

Please avoid sending identity documents or sensitive financial records unless they are specifically necessary to address the request.
