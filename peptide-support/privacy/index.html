1. Introduction

Welcome to Peptide Support, a mobile application developed and provided by FT PVT. LIMITED ("we," "us," or "our").

We value your privacy and recognize the importance of protecting personal and health-related information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Peptide Support accesses, processes, stores, and handles information, including personal tracking records, application preferences, reminders, and information voluntarily entered by users.

Peptide Support is designed to process user-entered tracking information locally on the user's device. FT PVT. LIMITED does not collect or store users' personal tracking information on company-operated servers.

2. Information Processed by the Application

Peptide Support provides functionality to organize and manage personal peptide-related tracking information.

Depending on the features used, information processed within the application may include:

  • Peptide-related tracking records.
  • Dosage information and schedules.
  • Weight-related tracking information.
  • Reminder dates and times.
  • Application preferences and settings.
  • Other tracking information voluntarily entered by users.

Some of this information may be considered sensitive health-related information.

This information is used to provide the application's features and is handled locally on the user's device.

FT PVT. LIMITED does not receive or maintain copies of these tracking records on company-operated servers.

3. Data Collection and Local Storage

Peptide Support is designed to handle user-entered information locally.

Personal tracking information is stored and processed on the user's device rather than uploaded to a company-operated database.

FT PVT. LIMITED does not operate a cloud-based user tracking database for Peptide Support.

Locally saved information may remain on the device until it is modified, removed through an available application function, or deleted through Android's application-data controls.

Device backup and restoration services may operate separately under the user's device settings and the operating-system provider's practices.

4. How Information Is Used

Peptide Support processes locally entered information for purposes such as:

  • Maintaining personal tracking records.
  • Organizing dosage information.
  • Managing schedules and reminders.
  • Displaying previously entered information.
  • Maintaining user preferences.
  • Displaying locally scheduled notifications.
  • Supporting available application functions.

FT PVT. LIMITED does not use users' personal peptide tracking information for personalized advertising, advertising profiles, or the sale of personal information.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

FT PVT. LIMITED does not sell, rent, or share users' personal or health-related tracking records with third parties.

Peptide Support is not designed to automatically transmit user-entered tracking records to company-operated servers, advertising networks, or analytics services.

The application includes Android sharing functionality that may allow users to initiate a sharing action.

If a user voluntarily chooses to share information through another application, messaging service, or recipient, the information may be handled according to the selected recipient's privacy practices.

Such user-initiated sharing is separate from any automatic collection or sharing by FT PVT. LIMITED.

6. Application Permissions

Peptide Support includes Android permissions for supporting its application functionality.

Notifications: The application may request notification permission to display scheduled reminders and alerts. Users can manage notifications through Android settings.

Device Restart: The application includes support for receiving device restart events, which may help restore locally scheduled reminders.

Vibration: The application may use vibration when displaying supported notification alerts.

Internal Application Communication: The application includes a custom Android permission to protect communication between internal broadcast components.

These permissions support application functionality and do not, by themselves, establish that user information is transmitted externally.

7. Notifications and Reminders

Peptide Support includes local notification functionality to support reminders and relevant application events.

Reminder-related information may be processed on the device to schedule and display notifications.

Users can enable or disable notifications through their Android device settings.

Disabling notifications may prevent reminders from appearing but does not necessarily remove locally saved tracking information.

Users should not rely exclusively on app notifications for important healthcare or medication decisions.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

User-entered tracking information is handled locally on the device.

Information may remain in local application storage while the application and its stored data are retained.

Users can manage local application storage using available application features and Android device settings.

Clearing application data generally removes the application's locally stored records and preferences.

Uninstalling the application generally removes app-specific local storage, subject to device backup, restoration, and operating-system behavior.

Users should preserve any information they wish to keep before clearing application data or uninstalling the application.

FT PVT. LIMITED does not maintain a company-operated cloud database of users' peptide tracking records requiring server-side deletion.

12. Data Security

We recognize that health-related tracking information may be sensitive.

Peptide Support uses local application functionality and Android storage mechanisms to handle user-entered information.

The security of locally stored information also depends on the user's device, operating-system configuration, and security settings.

Users are encouraged to:

  • Protect their devices using screen-lock security.
  • Keep their operating systems updated.
  • Avoid sharing sensitive information with untrusted recipients.
  • Review application permissions and device settings.

No electronic storage method or security system can be guaranteed to provide absolute protection.

13. Children's Privacy

Peptide Support is intended for adults aged 18 years and older and is not designed or intended for children under 18.

FT PVT. LIMITED does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

User-entered tracking information is handled locally on the device and is not collected or stored on company-operated servers.

Parents or guardians with concerns regarding a child's use of the application may contact us using the contact information provided below.

14. Health Information and Medical Disclaimer

Peptide Support is intended for personal tracking, organization, and informational purposes.

The application is not intended to provide medical diagnosis, prescribe treatment, or replace professional medical advice.

Information displayed by the application should not be considered a substitute for guidance from a qualified healthcare professional.

Users should consult an appropriate healthcare professional regarding medication use, dosage decisions, treatment plans, medical conditions, or other health-related concerns.

Users should not start, stop, or change prescribed treatment solely on the basis of information displayed by the application.

15. User Choices and Privacy Rights

Users can manage information entered into the application using available features and device settings.

Depending on the available functionality, users may:

  • Review or modify locally saved tracking information.
  • Manage reminder settings.
  • Enable or disable notification permissions.
  • Clear locally stored application data.
  • Decide whether to share information with external applications.

Users may also contact FT PVT. LIMITED with privacy-related questions or requests.

Where applicable privacy laws provide additional rights, requests will be reviewed in accordance with those requirements and the information actually held by FT PVT. LIMITED.

16. External Links and Applications

Peptide Support may allow users to open external websites, email applications, or other services.

These external services operate independently and may follow their own privacy practices.

FT PVT. LIMITED does not control how independently operated services collect, use, store, or disclose information voluntarily provided to them.

Users are encouraged to review the applicable privacy policies before providing personal or sensitive information to external services.

17. International Data Processing

Peptide Support does not use a company-operated cloud database to store or synchronize user-entered peptide tracking records.

Information voluntarily shared with external applications or services may be processed in different countries under those providers' own privacy practices.

The company does not control the independent processing locations or practices of external services selected by users.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
