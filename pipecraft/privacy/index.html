1. Introduction

FT PVT. LIMITED provides PipeCraft, an Android application for plumbing and piping calculations and related field-work activities.

This Privacy Policy explains how PipeCraft accesses and processes information, how local storage and user-selected sharing work, and how information is handled when you contact us.

Application: PipeCraft
Developer: FT PVT. LIMITED
Website: https://ftpvtlimited.github.io/
Contact Email: kcorporation70@gmail.com

2. Scope

This policy applies to information handled through PipeCraft and communications you send to FT PVT. LIMITED about the application.

Independent applications and services you choose for sharing, document storage, email, or printing operate under their own privacy practices.

3. Information You Enter or Import

PipeCraft processes information you choose to enter, select, or import. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Measurements, dimensions, angles, offsets, slopes, and fabrication allowances.
  • Calculation inputs, results, and reference dimensions.
  • Project names, customer details, site details, and drawing references.
  • Technician or operator names and work notes.
  • Planned records, checked records, and field measurements.
  • Project photos and selected files.
  • Reference records and backup content.
  • Preferences such as language, units, precision, and appearance.

Some information you enter may identify customers, workers, or other people, or contain confidential project details. Enter and import only information you are authorized to use.

Entering a customer name or site address does not give PipeCraft access to your device’s contacts or location services.

4. How Information Is Used

PipeCraft processes information to provide the features you choose, including:

  • Performing measurement and engineering calculations.
  • Displaying results and supporting diagrams.
  • Organizing project and field-work information.
  • Using imported fitting and pipe-reference records.
  • Applying your measurement, language, and display preferences.
  • Preparing reports, exports, backups, and print output.
  • Processing content from files you select.

If you contact us, we use the information you provide to address your enquiry.

5. Local Processing and Storage

PipeCraft handles its working data locally on your device. It uses on-device storage for preferences, reference content, and working records.

FT PVT. LIMITED does not automatically receive your calculations, project records, notes, measurements, reference files, or photos through an app-operated server. PipeCraft does not use a developer-operated cloud synchronization service for this working data.

Local work profiles and records are not online accounts held on our servers. We cannot remotely access information that exists only in your app’s local storage.

Keeping information locally does not make it anonymous or prevent someone with access to your device from obtaining it.

6. Camera Permission

PipeCraft requests camera permission when you choose the project-photo feature.

Camera access supports the photo-related action you initiate. Granting this permission does not, by itself, mean that photographs are uploaded to FT PVT. LIMITED.

You can decline the permission request or revoke camera access through Android Settings. Features requiring the camera will be unavailable while permission is denied.

Before attaching or sharing a photo, consider whether it contains people, customer information, confidential documents, or other sensitive details.

7. Selected Files and Document Access

When you import or export information, Android may display a system document picker so you can choose the relevant file or destination.

Selecting a document does not give PipeCraft unrestricted access to every file on your device.

If you select a cloud-based document provider, that provider may download, upload, or store the selected information according to its own practices. This processing is separate from PipeCraft’s local calculations.

8. Sharing, Exporting, Printing, and Backups

Information can leave PipeCraft when you choose to share, export, print, copy, or save a backup to another destination.

The selected output may contain measurements, calculation results, project details, names, notes, photos, or other information included in the relevant record.

Review the output and recipient before proceeding.

The receiving application, email service, storage provider, printing service, or person you select handles that information under its own practices. FT PVT. LIMITED does not control independent recipients or their retention and deletion processes.

A service you select may process information outside your country. Its processing location and safeguards depend on that service.

9. Clipboard

When you use a copy action, the selected information is placed on your device’s system clipboard.

Clipboard content may include measurements, reference records, or exported text. Its availability to other applications depends on your Android version, device settings, and subsequent actions.

Avoid copying confidential information unless you intend to use it outside the current field or screen.

10. Advertising, Analytics, and Crash Reporting

PipeCraft does not automatically send your locally handled working records to FT PVT. LIMITED for advertising, analytics, or remote crash reporting.

The application does not provide an automatic upload of your work to us.

User-selected sharing, external document providers, and support emails are separate activities described in this policy.

If a future version introduces additional services or different information-handling practices, this policy will be updated accordingly.

11. Third-Party Applications and Services

When you choose an external application or service to receive, store, send, or print information, that provider processes the information involved in your action under its own terms and privacy policy.

The provider depends on your selection. PipeCraft does not prescribe a single recipient for these actions.

The use of local software libraries for calculations, storage, document generation, or file handling does not by itself mean that your information is transmitted to the developers of those libraries.

12. Retention and Deletion

Information Stored Within the App

Local information remains in app storage until it is removed through available app controls, Android’s Clear storage/Clear data option, or removal of the application.

Clearing app storage can permanently remove local records and preferences. Consider whether you need an export before clearing data or uninstalling the app.

FT PVT. LIMITED cannot remotely retrieve or erase information that exists only on your device.

Exported and Shared Copies

Exported reports, backups, printed documents, clipboard content, and copies sent to other applications or people are separate from the app’s internal storage.

Deleting a record from PipeCraft, clearing app storage, or uninstalling the app does not automatically remove those copies.

Manage exported files through their storage location. Contact recipients or service providers about copies under their control.

Support Correspondence

Support emails are separate from PipeCraft’s local storage. Clearing or uninstalling the app does not delete emails you have sent.

You may contact us to ask about the handling or retention of your correspondence or request its deletion. Applicable legal requirements and exceptions depend on the circumstances.

13. Support Communications

If you email kcorporation70@gmail.com, we receive the sender address, message, and attachments you choose to provide.

Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information, confidential project records, customer details, or photographs in a support request.

Your email provider also processes your message under its own terms and privacy practices.

Contacting support does not automatically give us access to your local PipeCraft records. Information included in your message is information you choose to send.

14. Your Privacy Choices

You can:

  • Decide what information to enter or import.
  • Allow, deny, or revoke camera permission through Android Settings.
  • Choose files and document providers for import and export.
  • Decide whether to share, print, copy, or back up information.
  • Review recipients and output before disclosure.
  • Manage local app storage and separately stored copies.
  • Contact us about information you have sent directly to us.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, or other handling of personal information.

For information held only on your device, these choices generally need to be exercised through the app, Android Settings, or the relevant storage provider. For information you have sent to us, contact kcorporation70@gmail.com.

15. Children’s Privacy

PipeCraft is intended as a technical tool for plumbing and piping calculations and related work, rather than as a service directed to children.

This policy’s explanation of local processing and user-selected sharing applies to anyone using the application.

If you believe a child has sent personal information to FT PVT. LIMITED through support communications, contact kcorporation70@gmail.com so we can review and address the matter under applicable requirements.

16. Security

Local processing reduces the need to transmit working data to us, but it does not eliminate risks such as device loss, unauthorized access, accidental disclosure, or sharing with an unintended recipient.

Protect your device, review its access settings, and choose export and backup destinations carefully.

No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. This policy does not promise that every local record, exported document, or backup is encrypted.

17. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, information-handling practices, or relevant requirements change.

The effective month and year at the beginning of this policy identify its effective date. Updated versions will be made available on our website.
