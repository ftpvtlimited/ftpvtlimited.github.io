Website Privacy Policy

This policy applies to visits to our website and contact with our team.

Last updated: September 25, 2026

On this page

1. Scope2. Information we receive3. Cookies and analytics4. Use and sharing5. Retention and security6. Your choices and requests7. Children8. External links9. Updates and contact

1. Scope

This policy describes information handled when you visit the FT PVT. LIMITED website or contact us. Each app has its own privacy policy. Use the app links on this page for the relevant disclosures.

2. Information we receive

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Please share only what is needed for your enquiry.

The hosting and email providers used to deliver this website and respond to messages may process technical data, such as IP address, browser information, request time and security logs.

3. Cookies and analytics

This website does not include advertising, analytics trackers, account registration or a document-upload feature. Its own code does not set cookies or use browser storage. Your browser and our hosting or email providers may process technical information needed to deliver their services.

4. Use and sharing

We use support correspondence to respond to your request, investigate problems, maintain relevant records and protect against misuse. Relevant service providers process information needed to deliver hosting and email. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect lawful rights and safety.

We do not sell support correspondence. We do not use the website to collect the documents stored on your device.

5. Retention and security

We retain correspondence as reasonably necessary to handle the enquiry, resolve disputes, prevent abuse and meet applicable obligations. Provider logs follow the provider’s own retention practices. No transmission or storage system is completely secure; avoid sending sensitive files by email.

6. Your choices and requests

You may contact kcorporation70@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of personal information held directly by us. Applicable rights and exceptions depend on your location and the law. We may need to verify a request before acting.

Service providers may process data outside your country under their own terms and safeguards. Where applicable, you may raise a concern with your local data-protection authority.

7. Children

This website is intended for general product information and support. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can review and respond.

8. External links

Links to external websites and email services are governed by their operators’ policies. This website’s policy does not replace the privacy disclosures for individual apps or their third-party services.

9. Updates and contact

We will update the date on this page when this policy changes. For website privacy questions, contact FT PVT. LIMITED at kcorporation70@gmail.com.
