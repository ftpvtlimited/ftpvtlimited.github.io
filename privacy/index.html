1. Scope and who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how FT PVT. LIMITED handles information when you browse this website or contact us. The website presents our app collection, product details, availability notices and support information. This policy applies to the website only. Each app has its own policy, available from its app detail page; installing or using an app may involve different information and third-party services.

2. Information involved in a website visit

When you request a page, the hosting infrastructure receives information needed to deliver it, which may include your IP address, browser and operating-system information, request time, requested address and security-related logs. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes hosting information under its own privacy practices. Read the GitHub Privacy Statement for provider information. We do not ask you to create a website account or upload documents.

3. Information you send by email

Contact buttons open your email application. No message is sent until you choose to send it. If you email kcorporation70@gmail.com, we receive your address, the message, any name you include and attachments you choose to send. Our mailbox uses Gmail, and your email provider also handles the communication under its own practices. Please send only information needed for your enquiry, and avoid passwords, full payment details or confidential documents.

4. Search, sliders and Coming Soon pages

App search, category filters and slider navigation run in your browser. Our website code does not send your search text or slider interactions to an analytics service. Coming Soon buttons open a page on this website and may include an app identifier and store choice in the page address. This does not create a subscription, take payment or register you for launch notifications. The requested address may appear in browser history and hosting logs.

5. Cookies, browser storage and external content

The current website code does not set cookies or store preferences in localStorage or sessionStorage. We have not added advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, embedded social feeds or third-party fonts. Your browser may cache website files to load them faster. Hosting and email providers may process technical information as part of their own services. The footer social icons are non-clickable decorative graphics and do not connect to social networks.

6. Why we use information

We use information you provide to answer questions, offer support, investigate reported problems, handle privacy requests and maintain relevant correspondence. Technical information is used by the hosting infrastructure to deliver and secure the website. We may use information in our possession to prevent misuse, resolve disputes and comply with applicable obligations. Where privacy law requires a lawful basis, the relevant basis depends on the activity, including responding to your request, legitimate interests in support and security, or legal obligations. Where consent is required, it must be obtained separately.

7. Service providers and disclosure

Hosting and email providers handle information needed to supply their services. We may disclose information in our possession where required by applicable law or valid legal process, or where reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety and security. We do not sell support correspondence or use website interactions for targeted advertising. App providers and app SDKs are explained in the relevant app policies, not treated as website integrations merely because their names appear on an app page.

8. Retention and security

We keep support correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to address the enquiry, maintain necessary records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. There is no single fixed retention period for every message. Hosting logs and provider records follow the relevant provider’s practices. The website uses HTTPS, but no transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Avoid sending sensitive information that is unnecessary for support.

9. Your choices and privacy requests

You can browse without sending us an email. You can manage browser history, cached files and browser privacy settings yourself. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of information held directly by us, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact kcorporation70@gmail.com and describe your request. We may need proportionate information to verify it, and legal exceptions may apply. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. Provider-controlled records may require a request to that provider.

10. International processing and children

Our hosting and email providers may process information in countries other than where you live, subject to their applicable policies and safeguards. This website provides general company and product information and does not ask children to register an account or submit personal details. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us, contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

11. External links and app privacy

When you choose a link to an external website or a published app-store listing, the destination operator’s terms and privacy practices apply. A Coming Soon page stays on our website until an official external store link is available. For information about permissions, advertising, purchases or processing inside an app, open that app’s details from Our apps and follow its Privacy policy link.

12. Policy updates and contact

We will revise this policy and its update date when the website’s features or information-handling practices change. Where required, we will provide additional notice. For website privacy questions or requests, contact FT PVT. LIMITED at kcorporation70@gmail.com.
