Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use the App Android application, why it is processed, who may receive it, and the privacy choices available to you.

Provided by: FT PVT. LIMITED

Effective Date: October 2026

Privacy contact: kcorporation70@gmail.com

Last Updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use the App Android application (the “App”), why it is processed, who may receive it, and the privacy choices available to you.

In this Privacy Policy, “FT PVT. LIMITED,” “we,” “us,” and “our” mean FT PVT. LIMITED.

1. Summary

QR codes, barcodes, camera frames, and user-selected images are processed on your device for scanning and code creation. We do not operate a server that receives your scanned or created code content.

Scan history, created codes, favorites, and App settings are stored locally on your device. Android backup or device-transfer services may copy this local data if backup is enabled.

The free version displays advertisements through Google AdMob. Google may automatically collect device, identifier, approximate-location, and advertising-interaction data as described in this Policy.

Google Analytics for Firebase is used to understand App usage, Firebase Crashlytics is used to diagnose crashes and technical problems, Firebase Cloud Messaging is used to deliver push notifications and messages, and Firebase Remote Config is used to manage certain App configuration values and features remotely.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), also referred to as the Google Consent Form, is is used to request and manage applicable privacy and advertising consent where required.

Google Play processes purchases. FT PVT. LIMITED does not receive your complete payment-card or bank-account details.

You can manage permissions, advertising consent, notifications, and device advertising settings. You can also contact us regarding applicable privacy rights.

2. About the App

The App allows you to:

scan QR codes and barcodes using the device camera;

scan a code from an image you select;

create QR codes and barcodes using information you enter;

view scan results and take user-selected actions, such as opening a link, searching for a product, calling a number, preparing an email or message, adding a contact or calendar event, connecting to a Wi-Fi network, copying content, or sharing content;

store scan and creation history locally and mark entries as favorites;

save generated code images to your device or share them;

configure sound, vibration, and clipboard preferences;

receive push notifications and messages where enabled; and

purchase an optional one-time ad-free upgrade through Google Play.

The App does not require an account or login.

3. Information You Provide or Select

Code content

When you create a QR code or barcode, you may enter text, website addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, messages, Wi-Fi network details, social-profile links, or contact information.

The App processes this content locally to generate the code and may store it in local history. FT PVT. LIMITED does not receive this content.

Selected images and contacts

When you choose an image for scanning, Android’s system picker gives the App access to that selected image.

When you intentionally choose a contact for creating a contact code, the system contact picker gives the App access to the contact you select.

The App does not request unrestricted access to your full contact list or full photo library for these functions.

Clipboard

The App may place a scan result on the system clipboard when you use the copy function or enable automatic copying.

Information on the clipboard is managed by Android and may be accessible to other apps subject to Android’s restrictions.

You can disable automatic copying in the App’s settings.

Communications

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to provide.

We use this information to respond, provide support, address privacy requests, and improve the App.

5. Information Collected Automatically by Third Parties

Third-party SDKs integrated into the App may automatically collect or receive:

device manufacturer and model, operating-system version, language, time zone, screen characteristics, network type, mobile carrier, App version, and package information;

IP address and approximate location inferred from it, such as country- or city-level location;

Android Advertising ID, app-instance identifiers, Firebase installation identifiers, and other SDK-generated identifiers;

App launches, sessions, screens viewed, engagement time, first-open, update, purchase, and feature-interaction events;

ad requests, impressions, clicks, conversions, and other advertising interactions;

install source or campaign attribution information;

crash reports, stack traces, device state, App state, and related diagnostics; and

consent choices and consent strings.

The App does not request or use GPS or other device-based precise location for its normal operation. An IP address may nevertheless reveal approximate location.

The exact information processed by each Google service depends on its SDK version, configuration, consent status, device settings, Android version, region, and whether personalized advertising is permitted.

6. Camera, Images, and Scan Content

With your permission, the App uses the camera to display a live preview and detect codes.

Camera frames and selected images are analyzed on the device using Google ML Kit barcode-scanning technology.

The App does not record audio or video and does not send camera frames, selected images, or decoded scan content to FT PVT. LIMITED servers.

The App may send limited technical information associated with Google ML Kit to Google as described in Google’s documentation and terms.

This does not mean that FT PVT. LIMITED receives your camera images or decoded code content.

Scan content leaves the App only when you choose an action. For example:

opening a link sends the address to your browser or the relevant app;

searching for a product sends the product code to the selected search service;

calling, messaging, emailing, adding an event or contact, or sharing passes the relevant content to the app you select;

connecting to Wi-Fi passes the network details to Android’s Wi-Fi functionality; and

copying places the content on the Android clipboard.

The receiving app, website, search engine, or service processes that information under its own privacy policy.

8. Advertising

The free version of the App displays advertising, which may include banner, interstitial, native, and app-open ads.

We use Google AdMob to provide advertising within the App.

When the Google Mobile Ads SDK is active, Google may collect information directly from your device, including the Advertising ID, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device and App information, consent status, and ad requests, impressions, clicks, installs, and conversions.

Google may use this information to:

deliver and render ads;

personalize ads where permitted;

measure advertising performance and attribute conversions;

limit how often an ad is shown;

create reports and calculate advertising payments; and

detect fraud, abuse, and security threats.

We do not provide Google AdMob with your scan history, camera images, selected images, or the content used to create codes.

Depending on your region, consent choices, and device settings, the App may show personalized ads or non-personalized or limited ads.

Non-personalized or limited ads may still use IP address, device information, limited identifiers, and contextual information for delivery, reporting, frequency capping, security, and fraud prevention.

If you purchase the ad-free upgrade, the App stops requesting and displaying ads.

Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging, and Remote Config may continue to operate as described in this Policy.

9. Consent Management

We use Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), also referred to as the Google Consent Form, to request, store, and communicate applicable privacy and advertising choices where required.

In the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland, advertising consent is presented through a Google-certified consent-management platform and, where applicable, the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework.

Where available, you may revisit your choices through the App’s Privacy Choices or Manage Consent option.

You may also manage the Android Advertising ID and Android ad-privacy settings through your device settings.

Refusing personalized advertising does not remove all advertising; the App may continue to show non-personalized or limited ads.

10. Firebase Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used and improve its functionality.

Firebase Analytics may collect app-instance identifiers, device and App information, approximate location inferred from IP address, install source, sessions, screen views, engagement, purchases, advertising events, and feature interactions.

We use this information to measure audience size and retention, understand feature usage, assess advertising and purchase performance, identify areas for improvement, and measure acquisition campaigns.

We do not intentionally send scan content or created-code content to Firebase Analytics.

11. Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics is a diagnostic service used to identify and resolve crashes, errors, stability problems, and performance-related technical issues.

It may collect crash logs, stack traces, Firebase and Crashlytics installation identifiers, device model, operating-system version, App version, memory and execution state, and related diagnostic information.

We use Crashlytics data only for crash reporting, error diagnostics, improving App stability and performance, and identifying, prioritizing, and fixing technical problems.

We do not intentionally add scan content or created-code content to crash reports.

12. Firebase Cloud Messaging

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver push notifications and messages to users.

Notification permission is optional where Android requires it, and you can deny or disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

We do not use FCM to transmit your scan history, camera images, selected images, or created-code content.

13. Firebase Remote Config

We use Firebase Remote Config to manage certain App configuration values, settings, or features remotely without requiring an App update.

Remote Config may be used, for example, to adjust the availability or behavior of App features.

We do not use Remote Config to transmit your scan history, camera images, selected images, or created-code content.

14. Purchases and Google Play Billing

The App may offer a one-time purchase that removes advertisements.

Google Play processes the transaction under Google’s terms and privacy policy.

The App may receive a product identifier, purchase token, transaction or order reference, purchase time, status, and entitlement information to complete or restore the purchase.

FT PVT. LIMITED does not receive your complete payment-card number, bank-account details, or full payment credentials.

Google may provide developer financial reports containing information such as product, price, currency, country, date, and order reference for accounting, tax, refund, and fraud-prevention purposes.

17. Data Retention and Deletion

Local history, favorites, code content, and settings remain on the device until you delete them, clear the App’s storage, or uninstall the App, subject to Android backup and device-transfer copies.

Images saved to the photo gallery remain until you delete them.

Firebase Analytics data is retained according to our configured Google Analytics retention setting and Google’s applicable retention rules. Aggregated reports may be retained for longer.

Firebase Crashlytics retains crash stack traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems, according to Google’s published Firebase privacy information.

Google retains advertising-related information according to its policies and configured retention periods.

Google retains purchase and payment records according to its policies and applicable legal obligations. We retain any reports available to us only as long as reasonably necessary for accounting, tax, refunds, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, and legal compliance.

Support emails are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, and comply with law.

When information under our control is no longer reasonably necessary, we delete it or anonymize it, subject to legal requirements and backup-deletion cycles.

Because the App does not create user accounts, Google Play’s account-deletion requirement does not apply.

You can remove local App data as described above and may contact us to request deletion of personal information under our control.

18. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information, including encrypted network connections where supported, data minimization, permission minimization, access controls for developer consoles and support communications, SDK and dependency maintenance, and review of privacy and security configurations.

Local information is also protected by your device and account security.

You should keep your device updated and protected by a secure screen lock.

No transmission, storage system, SDK, or security measure is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
