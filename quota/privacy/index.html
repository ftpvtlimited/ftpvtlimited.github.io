Introduction

FT PVT. LIMITED ("we", "our", or "us") provides the Quota mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Quota accesses and processes, why that information is needed, how it is stored, and the choices available to you.

Quota is designed primarily to process and store information locally on your device.

1. Information Quota Accesses and Processes

Quota may access and process the following information only for the features described below.

Mobile and Wi-Fi Data Usage Information

Quota accesses Android network usage information to calculate and display how much mobile data and Wi-Fi data has been used.

This may include:

  • Mobile data received and sent
  • Wi-Fi data received and sent
  • Usage totals for supported applications
  • Usage dates and history
  • Data usage associated with application package names

This information is used to provide Quota's data-monitoring and usage-management features.

Application Usage Information

With Android Usage Access permission, Quota may access information necessary to determine which applications have consumed network data.

Quota may process application package names together with their mobile or Wi-Fi data consumption.

Quota does not use this access to collect the content you view or enter inside other applications.

SIM and Subscription Information

Quota uses Android phone and subscription-related permissions to support SIM-based data tracking.

Depending on the Android device and operating-system version, Quota may access information such as:

  • SIM/subscription identifiers used by Android
  • Carrier or network operator information
  • Basic SIM status
  • Information required to distinguish between SIM subscriptions

This access is used to associate data usage and bundle information with the appropriate SIM.

Quota does not use this functionality for advertising or tracking.

Information You Enter Into Quota

You may enter information such as:

  • A custom SIM name
  • Mobile data bundle allowance
  • Bundle price
  • Data-plan or usage settings

This information is used to calculate usage, remaining allowance, forecasts, and related information within the app.

2. Local Storage

Quota stores information required for its features locally on your device.

This may include:

  • Data usage history
  • Per-application usage records
  • SIM/subscription-related configuration
  • Carrier information
  • Data bundle settings
  • Bundle prices
  • Application preferences and settings

Quota uses local Android storage technologies, including an on-device SQLite database and local preference storage.

The analyzed version of Quota does not request the Android INTERNET permission. Accordingly, the app itself does not use an Internet connection to upload its locally stored usage information to our servers.

3. Permissions Used by Quota

Quota uses permissions required for its functionality.

Usage Access

Quota uses Android Usage Access and network statistics functionality to determine data usage associated with applications on your device.

This permission is necessary for Quota's core data-usage monitoring features.

Phone and SIM State

Quota declares phone-state permissions, including:

  • READ_PHONE_STATE
  • READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE

These permissions support SIM and subscription detection required for SIM-specific data monitoring.

Notifications

Quota may request notification permission so that it can display local alerts related to data usage, bundle limits, or other configured usage notifications.

Boot and Background Processing

Quota uses Android background scheduling functionality so that supported monitoring and notification features can continue to operate reliably.

The app may receive the device boot-completed event to restore or resume required background work after a device restart.

Vibration and Wake Lock

Quota may use vibration for notifications and Android wake-lock functionality temporarily when required for scheduled background processing.

These permissions are not used to collect personal information.

4. Notifications

Quota can provide local notifications related to your data usage and configured bundle information.

The analyzed app does not contain Firebase Cloud Messaging or another detected remote push-notification service.

You can control notification permission through your Android device settings.

5. Third-Party Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

The analyzed version of Quota does not contain detected advertising, analytics, attribution, or tracking SDKs.

No integrations were detected with services such as:

  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Meta Audience Network
  • AppLovin MAX
  • Mintegral
  • AppsFlyer
  • Adjust
  • Advertising mediation services

Quota does not use a detected advertising SDK or advertising identifier integration in the analyzed application.

6. Firebase and Cloud Services

The analyzed version of Quota does not contain detected integrations with Firebase services such as:

  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Firebase Remote Config
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Firebase Storage

No Quota cloud account or cloud synchronization functionality was detected in the analyzed application.

7. Information Quota Does Not Access for Its Detected Functionality

Based on the analyzed version of Quota, the app does not request access to:

  • Precise or approximate location
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Broad device file storage

The analyzed application also does not contain a detected user account or sign-in system.

8. Data Sharing

Quota's detected data-monitoring functionality is designed to operate locally on your device.

Because the analyzed version of Quota does not request Android Internet permission, the application itself does not transmit its locally stored usage records to FT PVT. LIMITED over the Internet.

We do not use detected advertising or analytics SDKs to share Quota usage information with advertising or analytics providers.

9. Feedback and Email

Quota may provide a feedback function that can open an email application installed on your device.

If you choose to send an email, any information that you voluntarily include will be transmitted using your selected email application and email provider.

Your email provider's own privacy practices apply to information sent through its service.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Quota keeps information needed for its data-usage and bundle-management features locally on your device.

The app provides functionality for removing locally stored usage records and configured bundle information.

You may also remove application data through Android system settings or by uninstalling Quota.

Uninstalling the app normally removes data stored in Quota's private application storage, subject to Android's normal operating-system behavior.

11. Data Security

Quota reduces privacy exposure by keeping its detected usage-monitoring information on the device and by not requesting Internet permission in the analyzed version.

However, no device or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should protect your device using appropriate security controls such as a screen lock and current operating-system security updates.

12. Children's Privacy

Quota does not contain a detected account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, social feature, or functionality designed to request personal information such as a child's name, email address, location, photographs, or contacts.

If the application's functionality or data practices change in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Quota's functionality, permissions, or data practices change.

Any revised Privacy Policy will reflect the functionality and privacy practices of the applicable version of Quota.

The effective date at the top of this Privacy Policy indicates when this version became effective.
