1. Introduction

FT PVT. LIMITED ("we," "our," or "us") provides SafeCite (the "App"), an Android application designed to support access to construction safety information, OSHA-related reference materials, and safety checklists.

We respect the privacy of our users and are committed to providing clear information about how the App accesses, processes, and handles information.

This Privacy Policy explains the App's local data processing, Android permissions, integrated third-party services, privacy considerations, and available contact options.

By using SafeCite, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Processed by the App

SafeCite includes locally stored construction safety reference information and checklist resources that support its functionality.

Depending on the features used, information handled by the App may include:

  • Locally stored application settings and preferences.
  • Safety reference materials and checklist-related information.
  • Application functionality and usage-related technical information.
  • Device and application information processed by integrated third-party services when applicable.
  • Purchase-related information if Google Play Billing functionality is made available.

The App does not require access to device contacts, SMS messages, precise location, camera, or microphone through the permissions identified in the reviewed APK.

The presence of third-party libraries does not necessarily mean that every data category described in their documentation is collected.

3. Local Data Processing and Storage

SafeCite is designed to support local processing of application information on the user's device.

The App contains locally packaged safety reference resources and uses Android-compatible local storage technologies.

Based on the developer's stated data-handling approach, application content and user-related information handled locally are not intentionally stored on FT PVT. LIMITED's own servers.

However, integrated third-party services may separately process technical or service-related information under their applicable policies when those services are active.

Local application data may also be subject to Android operating system storage and backup behavior, depending on the device's settings and application configuration.

4. Android Permissions

SafeCite declares permissions associated with the following functionality:

Internet and Network Access: Allows the App and integrated services to access network connections and determine connectivity status.

Billing: Supports possible purchase-related operations through Google Play Billing.

Advertising and Attribution: Advertising identifier, attribution, and related Android advertising API permissions are present to support compatible advertising integrations where active.

Background Operations: Foreground service and wake-lock permissions support certain Android background-processing capabilities.

Internal Application Components: Certain permissions are used to protect communication between application components.

These permissions do not automatically establish that personal information is accessed, collected, or transmitted.

Permission usage is subject to Android system controls and the applicable functionality of the App.

5. Firebase and Google Services

SafeCite includes integrations with certain Google Firebase services.

These integrations include Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Performance Monitoring, Firebase Remote Config, and Firebase Installations.

Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics may be used to understand application interactions and technical usage patterns when enabled.

The reviewed application configuration indicates that automatic Analytics collection is disabled by default.

If Analytics is subsequently enabled, information processed may include application interaction events, device-related information, and associated identifiers, depending on the applicable configuration.

Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics provides tools for identifying application crashes and technical problems.

The reviewed application configuration indicates that automatic Crashlytics collection is disabled by default.

When activated, Crashlytics may process crash reports, diagnostic information, and related device or application information.

Other Firebase Services

Firebase Performance Monitoring, Remote Config, and Firebase Installations are also integrated into the App.

Depending on their configuration and activation, these services may support performance diagnostics, remote application settings, installation identification, or related technical functions.

Information processed by Google services is subject to the applicable Google and Firebase privacy terms.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/

6. Advertising Services

SafeCite includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Android advertising-related permissions.

If advertising functionality is enabled, Google Mobile Ads may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, application interaction information, IP addresses, or ad performance information, according to Google's applicable policies and the configuration used.

Advertising may involve personalized or non-personalized experiences depending on applicable settings, user choices, and regional requirements.

The presence of the SDK does not establish that advertisements are displayed or that advertising data is currently transmitted.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Google Play Billing

SafeCite includes Google Play Billing integration.

If purchases or subscriptions are offered, transactions may be processed through Google Play according to its applicable terms and privacy practices.

FT PVT. LIMITED does not need to receive full payment-card information directly to use Google Play's billing system.

The availability of purchasable features depends on the App's actual production configuration.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

8. Data Sharing and Third-Party Processing

FT PVT. LIMITED's stated practice is not to transfer locally handled user content to its own servers for storage.

However, third-party services integrated with the App may independently collect or process technical information when enabled.

Such processing may involve Google services for analytics, diagnostics, performance measurement, advertising, or purchase-related functionality.

Third-party processing is governed by the relevant service configuration, its privacy terms, and applicable legal requirements.

FT PVT. LIMITED does not represent that the mere presence of an SDK establishes active collection or transmission of every data category supported by that SDK.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally may remain on the user's device until it is removed through application controls, Android storage settings, or other applicable device operations.

Removing the App may delete locally stored application information, subject to Android backup, restoration, and operating-system behavior.

Any information processed by active third-party services is subject to their applicable data retention and deletion practices.

Users may contact FT PVT. LIMITED regarding privacy-related questions or requests.

10. Data Security

We recognize the importance of protecting information handled through SafeCite.

The App uses Android platform functionality for local application processing and storage.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-party providers maintain their own security measures and responsibilities for information processed through their services.

11. User Choices and Controls

Users may manage applicable permissions, advertising preferences, application storage, and other available privacy controls through their Android device settings.

Where supported, users may also manage Google-related privacy and advertising choices through their Google account or device settings.

Users can contact us with questions about information associated with their use of SafeCite.

Certain technical services or features may depend on network availability or applicable device settings.

12. Children's Privacy

SafeCite provides construction safety and reference-related functionality.

Its intended audience is subject to the applicable Google Play target audience configuration.

Where the App is made available to children, FT PVT. LIMITED will need to ensure that any enabled advertising, analytics, and other third-party services are configured consistently with applicable children's privacy requirements.

Parents or guardians with privacy-related concerns may contact us using the information provided below.

13. International Data Processing

When third-party Google services are active, technical information processed by those services may be handled in countries other than the user's country of residence.

Such processing is subject to the applicable provider's privacy terms and relevant legal requirements.

14. Privacy Rights

Depending on the user's location and applicable law, users may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or other aspects of personal information processing.

Requests may be submitted through the contact address below.

We will review applicable requests in accordance with relevant legal obligations and the information actually available to us.

For information processed independently by third-party providers, users may also need to exercise available rights directly through those providers.
